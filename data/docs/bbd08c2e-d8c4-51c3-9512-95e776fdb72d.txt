L28: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L29: Section 505(o)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L30: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a signal of a serious risk of toxicity from drug over-exposure of rucaparib due to impaired hepatic function on the pharmacokinetics, or to assess a signal of a serious risk of toxicity from altered drug exposure of
L31: sensitive substrates of CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP3A4, and P-gp due to potential inhibition of concomitant rucaparib.
L32: Furthermore, the new pharmacovigilance system that FDA is required to establish under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks.
L33: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to assess these risks
L34: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following:
L35: 3141-3 Conduct a dedicated PK trial in patients with moderate hepatic impairment to determine the appropriate starting dose in patients with moderate hepatic impairment.
L36: The timetable you submitted on November 28, 2016, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following schedule:
L37: Final Report Submission: 12/2018
L38: 3141-4 Complete the ongoing drug-drug interaction trial CO-338-044 and submit the final study report.
L39: The timetable you submitted on November 28, 2016, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following schedule:
L40: Final Report Submission: 4/2017

L67: FDA has contracted with Eastern Research Group, Inc.
