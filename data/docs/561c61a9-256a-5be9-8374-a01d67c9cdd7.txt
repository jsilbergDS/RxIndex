L15: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to &lt;3 months because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because atopic dermatitis (AD) in infants &lt;3 months of age is difficult to diagnose.
L18: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study in subjects 3 to &lt;24 months because studies in patients 2 years of age and older are completed and ready for approval.
L19: Your deferred pediatric study required under section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act is required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. This required study is listed below.
L20: 4772-1 Conduct an efficacy, safety and pharmacokinetic (PK) study in pediatric subjects 3 to &lt;24 months with atopic dermatitis.
L21: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2025
L22: Study Completion: 06/2028
L23: Final Report Submission: 12/2028
L24: FDA considers the term *final* to mean that the applicant has submitted a protocol, the FDA review team has sent comments to the applicant, and the protocol has been revised as needed to meet the goal of the study or clinical trial.3
L25: Submit the protocol(s) to your IND 104601 with a cross-reference letter to this NDA. Reports of this required pediatric postmarketing study must be submitted as an NDA or as a supplement to your approved NDA with the proposed labeling changes you believe are warranted based on the data derived from this study. When submitting the reports, please clearly mark your submission "SUBMISSION OF REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS" in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the submission.
