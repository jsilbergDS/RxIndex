L6: Please refer to your Supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) dated May 31, 2018, received May 31, 2018, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Tudorza Pressair (aclidinium bromide Inhalation Powder), 400 mcg.
L7: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application (sNDA) provides for updates to the labeling for Tudorza Pressair (aclidinium bromide) to incorporate results of Study LAS-MD-45 (ASCENT), a post-marketing required safety study, including information regarding major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) and reduction in COPD exacerbations. This supplement also provides data for the effect of aclidinium on the St. George's Respiratory Questionnaire (SGRQ) score and labeling updates to comply with the requirements of the Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling Rule (PLLRR). The indication statement was simplified for consistency with current labeling practices.
