L17: ## DATING PERIOD
L18: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for PSMA-11 Ga 68 Injection is 3 hours from the End of Synthesis (EOS) when stored at 25°C (77°F); excursions are permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F). Store Ga 68 PSMA-11 Injection within the original container in radiation shielding.
