L17: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for ages 0 to 9 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because it is difficult to diagnosis bipolar disorder in children younger than 10 years and the prevalence rate of bipolar disorder for children younger than 10 years of age is low.
L20: We are deferring submission of your pediatric studies for ages 10 to 17 years for this application because pediatric studies should be delayed until additional safety or effectiveness data have been collected.
