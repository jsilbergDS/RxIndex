L25: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L26: Section 505(o)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L27: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to identify an unexpected serious risk of tumorigenicity of vanzacafor.
L28: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks.
L29: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following studies:
L30: 4734-1 Conduct a 2 year carcinogenicity study in rats administered vanzacafor.
L31: The timetable you submitted on December 2, 2024, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L32: Study Completion: 02/2025
Final Report Submission: 04/2025
L33: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to identify an unexpected serious risk of potential increase of systemic exposure of drugs which are sensitive substrates of breast cancer resistance protein (BCRP) due to the observed *in vitro* inhibitory profiles of vanzacafor and deutivacafor on BCRP.
L34: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following trials:
L35: 4734-2 Conduct a drug-drug interaction (DDI) clinical trial to assess the effect of vanzacafor/tezacafor/deutivacafor on the pharmacokinetics of a sensitive breast cancer resistance protein (BCRP) substrate.
L36: The timetable you submitted on December 17, 2024 states that you will conduct this trial according to the following schedule:
L37: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2025
Trial Completion: 05/2026
Final Report Submission: 11/2026
L38: FDA considers the term *final* to mean that the applicant has submitted a protocol, the FDA review team has sent comments to the applicant, and the protocol has been revised as needed to meet the goal of the study or clinical trial.3

L41: Section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) of the FDCA requires you to report periodically on the status of any study or clinical trial required under this section.
