L5: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received December 28, 2020, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Austedo (deutetrabenazine) 6 mg, 9 mg, and 12 mg Tablets.
L6: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for labeling changes to section 8.4 (Pediatric Use), obtained from studies completed in response to our Written Request issued January 8, 2020, regarding the use of deutetrabenazine in patients age 6 to 16 for the treatment of Tourette Syndrome. These changes include description of studies conducted, relevant safety information, and description of juvenile animal toxicity data.
L7: ## APPROVAL &amp; LABELING
L8: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.

L15: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
