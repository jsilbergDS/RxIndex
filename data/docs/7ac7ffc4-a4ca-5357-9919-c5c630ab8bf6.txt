L15: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: Because none of these criteria apply to your supplemental application, you are exempt from this requirement.
L18: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING COMMITMENT
L19: We have received your submission dated April 2, 2025, containing the final report for the following postmarketing commitment listed in the August 19, 2024, approval letter.
L20: 4681-2 Complete the MARIPOSA trial and include the results of the final overall survival analysis once the anticipated 390 death events have occurred in the amivantamab in combination with lazertinib and osimertinib arms to further characterize the clinical benefit of amivantamab in combination with lazertinib for the first line treatment of adult patients with metastatic NSCLC harboring EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R mutations.
L21: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above commitment was fulfilled.
L22: We remind you that there is a postmarketing requirement and a postmarketing commitment listed in the August 19, 2024, approval letter that are still open.
