L4: Dear Michelle Godin:
L5: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received June 18, 2024, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Invokana (canagliflozin) tablets.
L6: We also refer to your sNDAs dated and received June 18, 2024, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the FDCA for Invokamet (canagliflozin and metformin hydrochloride) tablets and Invokamet XR (canagliflozin and metformin hydrochloride) extended-release tablets.
L7: These Prior Approval sNDAs provide for the expansion of the approved indication as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus to include pediatric patients aged 10 years and older, with associated revisions to the Invokana, Invokamet, and Invokamet XR Prescribing Information and Medication Guides based on the results of the pediatric study 28431754DIA3018, entitled, *A Randomized, Multicenter, Double-Blind, Parallel-Group, Placebo-Controlled Study to Investigate the Efficacy and Safety of Canagliflozin in Children and Adolescents (\ge 10 to &lt;18 years) with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus.*
L8: This study was conducted to address the following postmarketing requirement (PMR) that was established under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA).
L9: 2027-2 A 26-week, randomized double-blind, placebo-controlled study, followed by a 26-week double-blind, placebo- or active-controlled extension, to evaluate the efficacy and safety of canagliflozin compared to placebo in pediatric patients ages 10 to &lt;18 years with type 2 diabetes mellitus, as add-on to metformin and as monotherapy.

L22: The SPL will be accessible from publicly available labeling repositories.

L30: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L31: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L32: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for ages 10 to less than 18 years for this application.
