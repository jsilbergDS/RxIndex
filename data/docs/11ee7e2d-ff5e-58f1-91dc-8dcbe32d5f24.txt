L21: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless these requirements are waived or your application is exempt from the requirement. Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
