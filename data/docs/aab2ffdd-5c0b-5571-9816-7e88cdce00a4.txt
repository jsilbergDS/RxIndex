L21: ## **DATING PERIOD**
L22: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Rivfloza (nedosiran) injection drug product in a glass vial (0.5 mL) shall be 36 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 2°C to 8°C, and for the drug product in prefilled syringes (0.8 mL and 1.0 mL) shall be 30 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 2°C to 8°C.
L23: ## **ADVISORY COMMITTEE**

L27: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L28: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L29: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
L30: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L31: Section 505(o)(3) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L32: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to identify an unexpected serious risk of carcinogenicity.
L33: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks.
L34: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following studies:
L35: 4522-1 Complete a 26-week GLP carcinogenicity study of nedosiran and murine surrogate (DCR-m355) by subcutaneous injection in TgRasH2 mice.
L36: The timetable you submitted on September 26, 2023, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L37: Draft Protocol Submission: 03/2023 (completed)
Final Protocol Submission: 10/2023
Study Completion: 11/2024
Final Report Submission: 01/2025
L38: 4522-2 Complete a long-term (2-year) GLP carcinogenicity study of nedosiran by subcutaneous injection in one rodent species. (To be conducted after results are available from a dose-range finding study.)
L39: The timetable you submitted on September 28, 2023, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L40: Draft Protocol Submission: 11/2024

L57: ## COMPENDIAL STANDARDS
L58: A drug with a name recognized in the official United States Pharmacopeia or official National Formulary (USP-NF) generally must comply with the compendial standards for strength, quality, and purity, unless the difference in strength, quality, or purity is plainly stated on its label (see FD&amp;C Act § 501(b), 21 USC 351(b)). FDA typically cannot share application-specific information contained in submitted regulatory filings with third parties, which includes USP-NF. To help ensure that a drug continues to comply with compendial standards, application holders may work directly with USP-NF to revise official USP monographs. More information on the USP-NF is available on USP's website7.
