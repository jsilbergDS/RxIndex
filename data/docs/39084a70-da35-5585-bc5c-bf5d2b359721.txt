L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for males of all age groups, females from birth to 12 years and for premenarchal adolescent females from 12 years to less than 18 years of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because bacterial vaginosis is not associated with these pediatric groups.
L22: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study in postmenarchal adolescent females ages 12 years to less than 18 years of age until March 2021, because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
L23: Your deferred pediatric study required under section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) is a required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(3)(C) of the FDCA. This required study is listed below.
L24: **3249-1:** Conduct an open label, multicenter, safety study of Solosec (secnidazole) oral granules in healthy postmenarchal adolescent females ages 12 years to less than 18 years of age with bacterial vaginosis.
L25: The timetable you submitted on September 6, 2017, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L26: Draft Protocol Submission:	12/2017
Final Protocol Submission:	03/2018
Study/Trial Completion:	03/2020
Final Report Submission:	03/2021
L27: Submit the protocol(s) to your IND 117811, with a cross-reference letter to this NDA.
L28: Reports of this required pediatric postmarketing study must be submitted as a new drug application (NDA) or as a supplement to your approved NDA with the proposed labeling changes you believe are warranted based on the data derived from these studies. When submitting the reports, please clearly mark your submission "**SUBMISSION OF REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**" in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the submission.
