L46: ## Summary:
L47: - The population studied was generally healthy and may not be representative of the patients likely to be prescribed a PDE-5 inhibitor in clinical practice (e.g.
L48: diabetics, or patients with cardiovascular disease). Any synergistic effect of silodosin and a PDE-5 inhibitor on blood pressure may be enhanced in patients with other co-morbidities.

- Although the maximum mean change in orthostatic vital sign parameters was similar among the three combination treatment groups, the number of positive orthostatic tests was greater when silodosin was combined with a PDE-5 inhibitor than with placebo. This was true both for subjects 45-64 years of age and those &gt;65 years.
- This study's small sample size limits the conclusions that can be drawn regarding the safety of silodosin combined with PDE-5 inhibitors, particularly in patients &gt;65 years (N=6).
