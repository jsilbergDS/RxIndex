L8: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics application provides for a new indication for the treatment, in combination with docetaxel, of patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer with disease progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy. Patients with EGFR or ALK genomic tumor aberrations should have disease progression on FDA-approved therapy for these aberrations prior to receiving CYRAMZA.

L19: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable.
