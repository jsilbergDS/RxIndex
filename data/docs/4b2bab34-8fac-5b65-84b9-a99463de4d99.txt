L4: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated May 12, 2005 received May 12, 2005, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act for Anthelios SX (2% avobenzene, 2% ecamsule, and 10% octocrylene) cream distributed by LaRoche-Posay.
L5: We acknowledge receipt of your submissions dated April 12, May 18, June 22 and 23, and July 10, 12, and 20, 2006.
L6: The May 18, 2006 submission constituted a complete response to our March 10, 2006 action letter.
L7: This new drug application is indicated to help the prevention of sunburn and to help provide protection from UVA and UVB rays.
L8: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the agreed-upon labeling text and with the minor editorial revisions listed below.
L9: - Replace [101-105] with “wavelengths” in every occurrence on the labels.
- Remove [101-105] from the third bulleted statement under *Uses* so that it reads, “helps provide protection from UVA rays (short and long wavelengths).”
L10: The final printed labeling (FPL) must be identical to, except for including the revisions listed, the enclosed labeling (immediate carton and container labels submitted July 20, 2006), and must be in the “Drug Facts” format (21 CFR 201.66). These revisions are terms of the NDA approval for the 3.4 oz. (100 g) product. Marketing the product before making the revisions, exactly as stated, in the product’s labeling and in the required format may render the product misbranded and an unapproved new drug.
L11: Please submit an electronic version of the FPL according to the guidance for industry titled *Providing Regulatory Submissions in Electronic Format - NDA*. Alternatively, you may submit 20 paper copies of the FPL as soon as it is available but no more than 30 days after it is printed. Individually mount 15 of the copies on heavy-weight paper or similar material. For administrative purposes, designate these submissions “**FPL for approved NDA 21-502.**” Approval of this submission by FDA is not required before the labeling is used.
