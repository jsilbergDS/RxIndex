L8: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling text.
L9: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L10: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L11: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
L12: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L13: Section 505(o) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute (section 505(o)(3)(A)).
L14: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to identify an unexpected serious risk of toxicity from a solvent, and to assess a signal of a serious risk of embryo-fetal toxicity, estrogenic/anti-estrogenic effects, hepatic impairment, Q-T prolongation and drug-drug interaction with ISTODAX®.
L15: Furthermore, the new pharmacovigilance system that FDA is required to establish under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA has not yet been established and is not sufficient to assess these serious risks.
L16: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following:
L17: 1556-1 Conduct a GLP embryo-fetal developmental reproductive toxicology study in rats to assess the embryo-fetal toxicity of romidepsin. The results from the rat study will determine if a study in a second species is warranted.
L18: The timetable you submitted on October 14, 2009, states that you will conduct this study according to the following timetable:
L19: Final Protocol Submission: July 31, 2010
L20: Study Completion Date: November 30, 2010
L21: Final Report Submission: June 30, 2011
L22: 1556-2 Conduct an animal study(ies) to determine the estrogenic/anti-estrogenic effects of romidepsin.
L23: The timetable you submitted on October 22, 2009, states that you will conduct the study(ies) according to the following timetable:
L24: Final Protocol Submission: April 30, 2010
L25: Study Completion Date: July 31, 2010
L26: Final Report Submission: October 31, 2010
L27: 1556-3 Conduct a GLP toxicology study in an appropriate animal species to characterize the toxicity profile of [REDACTED] (b)(4). The data from this study will be used in the justification of the acceptance criterion for [REDACTED] (b)(4) in romidepsin drug product administered IV on Days 1, 8 and 15 of a 28-day cycle.
L28: The timetable you submitted on October 14, 2009, states that you will conduct this study according to the following timetable:
L29: Final Protocol Submission: June 30, 2010
Study Completion Date: September 30, 2010
Final Report Submission: February 28, 2011
L30: 1556-4 Conduct an *in vitro* induction study using (b)(4) human hepatocytes to evaluate the effects of romidepsin on the 3 inducible forms of cytochrome P450 (CYP1A2, CYP3B6 and CYP3A4).
L31: The timetable you submitted on October 14, 2009, states that you will conduct this study according to the following timetable:
L32: Final Protocol Submission: April 30, 2010
Study Completion Date: July 31, 2010
Final Report Submission: October 31, 2010
L33: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to assess a signal of a serious risk of hepatic impairment, Q-T prolongation and drug-drug interaction.
L34: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following:
L35: 1556-5 Conduct a drug interaction clinical trial with a CYP3A4 inhibitor, ketoconazole, in patients with advanced cancer. This trial will be a crossover design to evaluate the effects of ketoconazole on the pharmacokinetic disposition of romidepsin.
L36: The timetable you submitted on October 14, 2009, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following timetable:
L37: Final Protocol Submission: July 31, 2010
Trial Completion Date: July 31, 2012
Final Report Submission: December 31, 2012
L38: 1556-6 Conduct a drug interaction clinical trial with a CYP3A4 inducer, rifampin, in patients with advanced cancer. This trial will be a crossover design to evaluate the effects of induction of CYP3A4 by rifampin on the pharmacokinetic disposition of romidepsin.
L39: The timetable you submitted on October 14, 2009, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following timetable:
L40: Final Protocol Submission: July 31, 2010
Trial Completion Date: July 31, 2012
Final Report Submission: December 31, 2012
L41: 1556-7 Conduct a clinical trial to determine the pharmacokinetics of romidepsin in advanced cancer patients with moderate and severe hepatic impairment. Submit the protocol for agency review prior to commencing the trial.
L42: The timetable you submitted on October 14, 2009, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following timetable:
L43: Final Protocol Submission: August 31, 2010
L44: Trial Completion Date: August 31, 2014
L45: Final Report Submission: August 31, 2015
L46: 1556-8 Perform trial GPI-06-0005 with adequate number of subjects to determine the potential of ISTODAX to prolong QT. The final analysis plan for the previously submitted protocol GPI-06-0005 will be provided. Exposure-response, central tendency and outlier analyses will be included in the final report.
L47: The timetable you submitted on October 27, 2009, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following timetable:
L48: Final Analysis Plan Submission: February 28, 2010
L49: Trial Completion Date: August 31, 2010
L50: Final Report Submission: March 31, 2011

L53: Section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) of the FDCA requires you to report periodically on the status of any study or clinical trial required under this section. This section also requires you to periodically report to FDA on the status of any study or clinical trial otherwise undertaken to investigate a safety issue. Section 506B of the FDCA, as well as 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii) requires you to report annually on the status of any postmarketing commitments or required studies or clinical trials.
L54: FDA will consider the submission of your annual report under section 506B and 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii) to satisfy the periodic reporting requirement under section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) provided that you include the elements listed in 505(o) and 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii). We remind you that to comply with 505(o), your annual report must also include a report on the status of any study or clinical trial otherwise undertaken to investigate a safety issue. Failure to submit an annual report for studies or clinical trials required under 505(o) on the date required will be considered a violation of FDCA section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) and could result in enforcement action.
