L16: ## DATING PERIOD
L17: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Vtama (tapinarof) cream shall be 36 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) with excursions permitted between 15 °C to 30°C (59 to 86°F).
L18: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L19: Your application for Vtama was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because evaluation of the safety data when used in the treatment of plaque psoriasis did not raise significant safety or efficacy issues that were unexpected in the intended population.
L20: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L21: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L22: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages \le 23 months because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because plaque psoriasis is uncommon in the lowest age groups and the diagnosis may be challenging.
L23: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages 2 to 17 years for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
