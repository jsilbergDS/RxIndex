L6: ## LICENSING
L7: We have approved your BLA for Andemby (garadacimab-gxii) effective this date. You are hereby authorized to introduce or deliver for introduction into interstate commerce, Andemby under your existing Department of Health and Human Services U.S. License No. 1767. Andemby is indicated for prophylaxis to prevent attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE) in adult and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older.
L8: ## MANUFACTURING LOCATIONS
L9: Under this license, you are approved to manufacture garadacimab-gxii drug substance at CSL Behring (Australia) Pty Ltd in Broadmeadows, Australia. The drug product will be manufactured and filled at CSL Behring GmbH in Marburg, Germany. The final drug product will be assembled, labeled and packaged at CSL Behring GmbH in Marburg, Germany for the prefilled syringe with needle safety device or at (b)(4) (b)(4) for the prefilled autoinjector. You may label your product with the proprietary name, Andemby, and market it in 200 mg/1.2 mL solution in single-dose prefilled autoinjector and in single-dose prefilled syringe with needle safety device.

L27: ## **ADVISORY COMMITTEE**
L28: Your application for Andembry was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because the application did not raise significant safety or efficacy issues in the intended population as the safety profile is similar to that of other drugs or biological products approved for HAE prophylaxis. Because there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion, convening an advisory committee meeting with outside expertise was not necessary.
L29: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L30: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L31: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
