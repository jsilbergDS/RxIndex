L4: Dear Ms. Gerry:
L5: Please refer to your Supplemental New Drug Applications (sNDAs) dated and received December 17, 2015 and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for PREZISTA® (darunavir) tablet, 75 mg, 150 mg, 600 mg, 800 mg (NDA 21976), and PREZISTA® (darunavir) oral suspension, 100 mg/mL (NDA 202895).
L6: These Prior Approval supplemental new drug applications propose the following changes:
L7: - To update the DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION and CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY, Pharmacokinetics sections of the labeling with data and results from a study investigating the use of darunavir during pregnancy and postpartum
- To update USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS sections 8.1 through 8.3 to be compliant with the “Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling Rule”
L8: In addition, the Agency proposed the following changes:
L9: - To remove WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS, Resistance/Cross-Resistance subsection
- To update DRUG INTERACTIONS section:Add clinical comments regarding the co-administration of omeprazole to Table 11.
- Create new section (section 7.4) to include drugs without clinically significant interaction with Prezista.
- Remove H2 blockers and antacids because of the absence of supporting information to provide clinical recommendation regarding co-administration with Prezista.
- Remove telaprevir information from DRUG INTERACTIONS, Table 11 and CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY, Table 17 because telaprevir is no longer marketed nor distributed in the United States.
- To update CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY section with addition of Figure 1 to display darunavir exposure changes in pregnant subjects by trimester.
L10: - To reformat and revise the PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION based upon the Agency's current standard.
- To reformat and revise the PATIENT INFORMATION based upon the Agency's current standard.
- To update the INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE.

L24: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L25: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
