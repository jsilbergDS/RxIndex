L8: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received November 22, 2024, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Spinraza (nusinersen) injection.
L9: We acknowledge receipt of your amendment dated October 3, 2025, which constituted a complete response to our September 22, 2025, action letter.
L10: This Prior Approval sNDA provides for revisions throughout the Prescribing Information (PI) regarding a completed clinical study [Study 4 (NCT04089566)], which provides an additional higher dosing regimen. Two corresponding additional strength presentations [28 mg/5 mL (5.6 mg/mL) and 50 mg/5 mL (10 mg/mL)] are included for administration of the new dosing regimen. This sNDA also includes revisions to the Pediatric Use subsection (8.4) of the PI to include results from a combined 6- and 13-week toxicity study in juvenile monkeys.
