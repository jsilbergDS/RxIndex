L10: Prior Approval sBLA S-042 proposes an update to the Adverse Reactions section (Section 6) of the Prescribing Information to remove pneumonia and peripheral edema Preferred Terms which were inadvertently included as Adverse Drug Reactions in the metastatic breast cancer population treated with Enhertu in DB-06.
