L3: Dear David Craig Zecher II:
L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug applications (sNDAs) dated and received December 12, 2023, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Onglyza (saxagliptin) tablets and Kombiglyze XR (saxagliptin and metformin hydrochloride) extended-release tablets.
L5: These Prior Approval sNDAs provide for revisions to section 8.4 *Pediatric Use* of the Onglyza and Kombiglyze XR Prescribing Information and Medication Guides based on the results of the pediatric study D1680C00019 (CV181375) entitled, A 26-week, *multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, parallel group, phase 3 trial* with a 26-week safety extension period evaluating the safety and efficacy of *dapagliflozin 5 and 10 mg, and saxagliptin 2.5 and 5 mg in pediatric patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus who are between 10 and below 18 years of age*. Additional edits were made throughout the Prescribing Information and Medication Guides for Onglyza and Kombiglyze XR to modernize with current labeling guidances, clarify language, improve readability and organization of information, and align with other products.
L6: This study was conducted to address the following postmarketing requirement (PMR) established in the April 24, 2017, Acknowledge New Postmarketing Requirement letter:
L7: 3199-1 Conduct a 26-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the monotherapies saxagliptin and dapagliflozin for the treatment of pediatric patients ages 10 to &lt; 18 years with type 2 diabetes mellitus, followed by a 26-week site- and subject-blinded safety extension period (weeks 26 to 52). Background therapy will consist of either metformin, insulin, or metformin plus insulin. A second randomization will take place at week 14, with upitration of dose (saxagliptin may be increased from 2.5 mg to 5 mg; dapagliflozin from 5 mg to 10 mg) for approximately half of the subjects with a hemoglobin A1C greater than or equal to 7%.
L8: We refer to our June 12, 2024, Fulfillment of Postmarketing Requirement letter under NDA 202293/S-031 and NDA 205649/S-022, which concluded the above requirement has been fulfilled.
