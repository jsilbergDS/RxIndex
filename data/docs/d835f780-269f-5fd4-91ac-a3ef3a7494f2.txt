L30: ## DATING PERIOD
L31: The dating period for binimetinib shall be 36 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 20-25°C (68-77°F) with excursions permitted between 15-30°C (59-86°F). The retest dating period for your drug substance shall be (b)(4) months from the date of manufacture when stored at below (b)(4)°C.
L32: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L33: Your application for binimetinib was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because this is not the first drug in its class and there were no novel issues identified that would benefit from the Advisory Committee's expertise.
L34: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L35: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L36: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
