L16: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L17: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L18: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for ages 2 years to 6 years for this application.
L19: ## **FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT**
L20: We have received your submission dated January 16, 2018, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the June 19, 2015 post approval postmarketing requirement letter.
L21: 2919-1 Deferred pediatric study under PREA for the treatment of HIV-1 infection in pediatric subjects from 2 years to 6 years of age. This study will determine the pharmacokinetic profile, safety, and activity of etravirine in pediatric subjects from 2 years to 6 years of age.

L23: This completes your postmarketing requirement acknowledged in our June 19, 2015 letter.
