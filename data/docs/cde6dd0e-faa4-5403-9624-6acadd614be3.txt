L9: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) received July 28, 2023, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Skyclarys (omaveloxolone) capsules.
L10: This Prior Approval sNDA provides for revisions to the Prescribing Information in the DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.2), CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY (12.3), and PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION (17) sections to allow for opening of capsules and sprinkling the contents onto applesauce for administration to patients who are unable to swallow whole capsules. Corresponding revisions were made to the Patient Information.

L22: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L23: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L24: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
