L35: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a known serious risk of the potential increase in systemic exposure of amisulpride in patients with severe renal impairment.
L36: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L37: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to assess a known serious risk of the potential increase in systemic exposure of amisulpride in patients with severe renal impairment.
L38: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following trial:
L39: 3874-4 A pharmacokinetic study following a single dose of Barhemsys (amisulpride) injection in patients with severe renal impairment and end-stage renal disease (i.e., eGFR &lt; 30 mL/min/1.73 m2) and healthy subjects as a control group.
