L17: BEST PHARMACEUTICALS FOR CHILDREN ACT
L18: Section 505A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 355c), also known as the Best Pharmaceuticals for Children Act (BPCA), allows the FDA to request pediatric studies for new drugs before approval.
L19: This supplemental application provides for pediatric labeling pursuant to BPCA. This product was studied for use in pediatric patients ages 2 years and older for the treatment of Rett syndrome.
L20: REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L21: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L22: Because none of these criteria apply to your supplemental application, you are exempt from this requirement.
