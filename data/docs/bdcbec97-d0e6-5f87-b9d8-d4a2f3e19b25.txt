L18: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages less than 2 years because the product does not represent a meaningful therapeutic benefit over existing therapies for pediatric patients in this age group **and** is not likely to be used in a substantial number of pediatric patients in this group. The request for waiver is justified in children less than 2 years of age based on the difficulty of definitive diagnosis of CSU in this age group, and the preferred use of other treatment options (e.g. antihistamines) in this age group prior to use of dupilumab.
L21: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages 2 to &lt; 12 years for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
