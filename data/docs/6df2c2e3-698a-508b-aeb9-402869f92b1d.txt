L8: ## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
L9: This review evaluates whether a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy (REMS) for the new molecular entity Enhertu (fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki) is necessary to ensure the benefits outweigh its risks. Daiichi Sankyo, Inc. submitted a Biologic Licensing Application (BLA) 761139 for fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki with the proposed indication for the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer who have received two or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting. The serious risks associated with fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki include interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, neutropenia, left ventricular dysfunction, and embryo-fetal toxicity. The applicant did not submit a proposed REMS but submitted a risk management plan with this application.
L10: DRM and Division of Oncology Products 1 (DOP1) agree that a REMS is not necessary to ensure the benefits of fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki outweigh its risks. The efficacy of fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki was supported by the DESTINY-Breast01 trial, in which the fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki group had a confirmed objective response rate of 60.3%. DOP1 recommends accelerated approval based on the currently available data. Labeling that includes a boxed warning and a Medication Guide will be used to communicate the serious risk of interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis and embryo-fetal toxicity. The other serious risks including neutropenia and left ventricular dysfunction will be communicated in the warnings and precautions section of the label.
L11: ## 1 Introduction
L12: This review evaluates whether a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy (REMS) for the new molecular entity (NME)a Enhertu (fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki) is necessary to ensure the benefits outweigh its risks. Daiichi Sankyo, Inc. submitted a Biologic Licensing Application (BLA) 761139 for fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki with the proposed indication for the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer who have received two or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting.1 This application is under review in the Division of Oncology Products 1 (DOP1). The applicant did not submit a proposed REMS with this application but submitted a risk management plan with this application.
L13: ## 2 Background
L14: ## 2.1 PRODUCT INFORMATION
L15: Enhertu (fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki), a NME, is a HER2-directed antibody and topoisomerase inhibitor conjugate, proposed for the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer who have received two or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting. Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki is a humanized anti-HER2 antibody attached to a topoisomerase 1 inhibitor (MAAA-1181a) by a cleavable linker. Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki is supplied as a 100 mg vial for IV injection. The proposed dosing regimen is fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-

L22: ## 3.1 DESCRIPTION OF THE MEDICAL CONDITION
L23: Breast cancer is a common cause of cancer in women worldwide.2,3 The estimated number of new cases of breast cancer in the United States in women and in men is 268,600 and 2670, respectively.4,c Furthermore, the estimated number of women with metastatic breast cancer in the United States in 2017 was 154,794.5 Metastatic breast cancer is currently not curable and the five year relative survival of distant breast cancer is 27.4%.3,6,d

L31: ## 4 Benefit Assessment
L32: The pivotal trial NCT 03248492 (DESTINY-Breast01) supporting this application for efficacy consisted of a Phase 2 multicenter open-label single arm trial which evaluated fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki in patients with HER2-positive, unresectable and/or metastatic breast cancer who had received two or more prior anti-HER2 therapies.1,13 In DESTINY-Breast01, 184 patients received fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki 5.4 mg/kg by intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. Patients had received prior trastuzumab and ado-trastuzumab emtansine and 66% of patients received prior pertuzumab. The primary endpoint was confirmed objective response rate (ORR). The fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki treatment group had a confirmed ORR of 60.3% (95% CI 52.9% to 67.4%) with a median duration of response of 14.8 months (95% CI 13.8 to 16.9). The FDA clinical reviewer recommended accelerated approval based on the currently available data.14,e
L33: ## 5 Risk Assessment &amp; Safe-Use Conditionsf

L39: The serious risks9 associated with fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki which include interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis, neutropenia, left ventricular dysfunction, and embryo-fetal toxicity are summarized in the sections below. A Medication Guide will also be used to inform patients of these risks.
L40: ## 5.1 INTERSTITIAL LUNG DISEASE / PNEUMONITIS
L41: The risk of serious and fatal interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis will be included in a boxed warning, and section 5 of the label as the first warning and precaution. Interstitial lung disease was reported in 9% of patients. The median time to onset of interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis was 4.1 months, with a range from 1.2 months to 8.3 months. There were 6 deaths (2.6% of patients) in the fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki group adjudicated as interstitial lung disease.14 The proposed label recommends monitoring for signs and symptoms of interstitial lung disease. It states to consider treatment with corticosteroids for Grade 1 interstitial lung disease and to promptly initiate treatment with corticosteroids for Grade 2 or greater interstitial lung disease.
L42: ## 5.2 NEUTROPENIA
L43: An adverse reaction of neutropenia occurred in 30% of patients who received fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki, with Grade 3 or 4 neutropenia reported in 16% of patients. If approved, this risk will be communicated in the warnings and precautions section of the label.
L44: ## 5.3 LEFT VENTRICULAR DYSFUNCTION
L45: Left ventricular dysfunction has been reported with anti-HER2 therapies. Two patients in the fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki group experienced asymptomatic decrease in left ventricular ejection fraction. If approved, this risk will be communicated in the warnings and precautions section of the label.

Important medical events that may not result in death, be life-threatening, or require hospitalization may be considered a serious adverse drug experience when, based upon appropriate medical judgment, they may jeopardize the patient or subject and may require medical or surgical intervention to prevent one of the outcomes listed in this definition.
L47: ## 5.4 EMBRYO-FETAL TOXICITY
L48: Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki may cause fetal harm based on the mechanism of action of the drug. No clinical data is available with fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki in pregnancy in humans. However, oligohydramnios has been reported with HER2-directed antibody in the postmarket setting. Furthermore, MAAA-1181a may cause embryo-fetal harm as it is genotoxic and targets actively dividing cells. The risk of embryo-fetal toxicity will be included in a boxed warning, a Medication Guide, and the warnings and precautions section of the label. The proposed label states advise patients of the risk of embryo-fetal harm and need for effective contraception. The proposed label recommends in females of reproductive potential to verify pregnancy status before starting fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki and that effective contraception be used during treatment and for at least 7 months after the last dose. In addition, in males with a female partner of reproductive potential it is recommended that effective contraception be used during treatment and for at least 4 months after the last dose.
L49: ## 6 Expected Postmarket Use
L50: If approved, fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki will primarily be used in both inpatient and outpatient (such as infusion centers) settings. The likely prescribers will be hematologists and oncologists.
L51: ## 7 Risk Management Activities Proposed by the Applicant
L52: The Applicant did not propose any risk management activities for fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki beyond routine pharmacovigilance and labeling.
L53: ## 8 Discussion of Need for a REMS
L54: The FDA clinical reviewer recommends approval of fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki on the basis of the efficacy and safety information currently available. The indication will be approved under accelerated approval based on tumor response rate and duration of response. Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki is a HER2-directed antibody and topoisomerase inhibitor conjugate and is a treatment option for patients with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer who have received two or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting. The efficacy of fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki was supported by the DESTINY-Breast01 trial, in which the fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki group had a confirmed ORR of 60.3%. The serious risk associated with fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki of interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis and embryo-fetal toxicity will be communicated in a boxed warning, a Medication Guide, and in the warnings and precautions section of the label. The other serious risks including neutropenia and left ventricular dysfunction will be communicated in the warnings and precautions section of the label.
L55: Breast cancer is a common cause of cancer in women worldwide. The estimated number of women with metastatic breast cancer in the United States in 2017 was 154,794. Metastatic breast cancer is currently not curable and the five year relative survival of distant breast cancer is 27.4%. Based on the efficacy and risk associated with fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki for the treatment of adult patients
L56: with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer who have received two or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting, the DRM and DOP1 recommendation is that a REMS is not necessary to ensure that the benefits outweigh the risks. Labeling that includes a boxed warning will be used to communicate the serious risk of interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis. The box warning states interstitial lung disease and pneumonitis, including fatal cases, have been reported with fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki. Monitor for and promptly investigate signs and symptoms including cough, dyspnea, fever, and other new or worsening respiratory symptoms. Permanently discontinue fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki in all patients with Grade 2 or higher interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis. Labeling also includes recommendations for treatment with corticosteroids for Grade 1 and Grade 2 or greater interstitial lung disease.
L57: ## 9 Conclusion &amp; Recommendations
L58: Based on the clinical review, the benefit-risk profile is favorable therefore, a REMS is not necessary for fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki to ensure the benefits outweigh the risks. At the time of this review, evaluation of safety information and labeling was ongoing. Please notify DRM if new safety information becomes available that changes the benefit-risk profile; this recommendation can be reevaluated.
