L145: ## NON-FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING COMMITMENT
L146: We refer to the following post-marketing commitment:
L147: 1268-1. Gilead agrees to conduct a study examining the effects of LETAIRIS on 6-minute walk distance at peak and trough plasma concentrations, and further agrees to reach agreement on an appropriate study design with the Division.
L148: Protocol Submission: by 10/1/2007
Study Start: by 06/2008
Final Report Submission: by 12/2009
L149: After reviewing your submission, we concluded that the data submitted as part of this supplement were not sufficient to meet the terms of this postmarketing commitment for the following reasons:
L150: The effect of ambresentan on 6MWD at peak and trough plasma concentration was evaluated in the combination arm by subtracting the effect of tadalafil. No evaluations in the ambresentan monotherapy arm were performed.
L151: After adjusting for the effect of tadalafil, 6MWD for the combination group was greater at trough (17.4 m) when compared to peak (7 m). As a numerically greater distance at trough when compared to peak is against the expectation, the study results are effectively inconclusive.
L152: In addition, there are issues with the design of this assessment -
L153: - It appears to be assumed that the subtraction of the tadalafil effect from the combination treatment effect represents ambresentan effect alone. This assumption is not verified.
- Baseline measurements of 6MWD were not time-matched to account for variations in 6MWD based on timing of the day.
L154: Therefore, this postmarketing commitment is not fulfilled and remains an open commitment.
