L15: ## DATING PERIOD
L16: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Redemplor (plozasiran) for subcutaneous injection shall be 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 5°C.
L17: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L18: Your application for Redemplor was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because evaluation of the safety data when used in the treatment of FCS did not raise significant safety or efficacy issues that were unexpected for a drug of this class. In addition, there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
L22: ## POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)
L23: Section 505(o)(3) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L24: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a signal of a serious risk of carcinogenicity.
L25: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L26: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following study:
L27: 4909-1 Complete the ongoing 2-year rat carcinogenicity study to assess the carcinogenic potential from long-term subcutaneous exposure to ARO-APOC3 (plozasiran).
L28: The timetable you submitted on October 31, 2025, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L29: Study Completion: December 2025
Final Report Submission: January 2026
L30: FDA considers the term *final* to mean that the applicant has submitted a protocol, the FDA review team has sent comments to the applicant, and the protocol has been revised as needed to meet the goal of the study or clinical trial.3
L31: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to assess a signal of a serious risk of chronic drug-induced liver injury and dysglycemia.
L32: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following trial:
L33: 4909-2 Complete the ongoing randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials evaluating plozasiran in patients with severe hypertriglyceridemia (AROAPOC3-3003 [SHASTA-3], including the magnetic resonance imaging proton density fat fraction substudy, and AROAPOC3-3004 [SHASTA-4]) and evaluate the potential for drug-induced liver injury and drug-induced dysglycemia (e.g., new-onset diabetes mellitus, worsening glycemic control in those with existing diabetes mellitus).
L34: The timetable you submitted on October 31, 2025, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following schedule:
L35: Trial Completion: December 2026
Final Report Submission: June 2027
