L13: In order to verify the clinical benefit of Aliqopa (copanlisib), submit the complete final clinical report and datasets from a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of Aliqopa in combination with immunochemotherapy versus immunochemotherapy alone in patients with relapsed follicular lymphoma, marginal zone lymphoma, small lymphocytic lymphoma, or lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma/Waldenström’s macroglobulinemia. The primary endpoint is progression-free survival. Enroll approximately 520 patients.

L19: SECTION C: PMR/PMC Rationale
L20: 1. Describe the particular review issue and the goal of the study4 or clinical trial5 in the text box below.
L21: Study 16349 Part B is a single-arm study evaluating Aliqopa (copanlisib) monotherapy that used a primary endpoint of objective response rate (ORR), which is a surrogate endpoint likely to predict clinical benefit. In the single-arm study 16349 Part B patients with relapsed follicular lymphoma achieved an ORR of 58.7% per independent review committee assessment and a median duration of response of 12.2 months. FDA has previously accepted objective response rates supported by duration of response from a single-arm trial as a basis for accelerated approval for Non-Hodgkin lymphoma therapies. The goal of this PMR is to obtain long-term efficacy outcomes including progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) from a randomized controlled clinical trial. Time-to-event endpoints, such as PFS and OS, cannot be adequately interpreted in single arm clinical trials due to confounding effects of the natural history of the disease.

L89: 6. Describe the particular review issue and the goal of the study13 or clinical trial14 in the text box below.
L90: The safety profile of Aliqopa (copanlisib) monotherapy was evaluated in single-arm studies in patients with relapsed follicular lymphoma, marginal zone lymphoma, small lymphocytic lymphoma, or lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma/waldenström macroglobulinemia. The clinical significance of adverse events associated with Aliqopa therapy cannot be fully evaluated using single-arm studies. Additionally, the median duration of exposure was 22.0 weeks (5.5 cycles), which is a short duration to evaluate safety. Nevertheless, the safety issues identified in the single-arm studies of Aliqopa include infections, hypertension, hyperglycemia, pneumonitis, neutropenia, gastrointestinal disorders, and severe cutaneous reactions. The goal of this PMR is to further characterize the safety profile of copanlisib in a randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial in patients with relapsed follicular lymphoma, marginal zone lymphoma, small lymphocytic lymphoma, or lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma/Waldenström's macroglobulinemia.

L226: 16. Describe the particular review issue and the goal of the study31 or clinical trial32 in the text box below.
L227: Hepatic metabolism and biliary excretion plays a major role in the elimination of copanlisib and its metabolites. There is no PK data to determine copanlisib dose for patients with moderate to severe hepatic impairment. Severe renal impairment can affect the renal clearance of copanlisib; it may also affect the expression of hepatic metabolism enzymes and transporters. Therefore, the biliary excretion of copanlisib and its metabolites may also be affected. A subpopulation of patients with follicular lymphoma is expected to have severe renal impairment (eGFR &lt; 30 mL/min/1.73m2). There is no PK data to determine copanlisib dose for patients with severe renal impairment. A clinical trial is needed to provide understanding for copanlisib dose adjustment for these patient subpopulations.

L299: 21. Describe the particular review issue and the goal of the study40 or clinical trial41 in the text box below.
L300: Copanlisib showed a concentration-dependent inhibition of renal transporter MATE2-K using metformin as a probe substrate. The estimated IC50 was 0.089 μM. This may translate into clinically relevant inhibition at therapeutic doses (\frac{[I]_1}{IC_{50}} = 11.1 with Cmax of 1 μM at recommended dose) according to the FDA guidance for drug interaction studies (2012). Metformin is a sensitive MATE2-K substrate used as a concomitant medicine in the pivotal trial to control hyperglycemia. It is also expected that metformin could be used in the management of copanlisib induced hyperglycemia in the clinical setting. Increase in exposure to metformin due to significant DDI may result in lactic acidosis. Therefore, a clinical trial is needed to provide dose adjustment recommendation for such concomitant medications.

L515: This was an open-label, uncontrolled, single arm Phase 2 study in eligible patients who received treatment with copanlisib at a fixed dose of 60 mg (0.8 mg/kg) prior to Study Protocol Amendment #4).
L516: The objective of Study 16349 Part B was to evaluate the efficacy and safety of copanlisib in patients with indolent B-cell Non-Hodgkin's lymphoma (NHL) relapsed after, or refractory to widely-used approved therapies in standard practice.
L517: The primary efficacy endpoint was the objective response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients who had a best response rating of complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) according to the Revised Response Criteria for Malignant Lymphoma. The Revised Response Criteria for Malignant Lymphoma incorporate concepts from the International Working Group (IWG) 1999 guidelines, as well as evaluation of extranodal disease, and before and after imaging comparison of positive mass lesions using more current imaging techniques using fluorodeoxyglucose (FDG) enhanced positron emission tomography (PET) before therapy versus FDG-PET or computerized tomography (CT) scan comparison of mass lesions after therapy.
