L5: Please refer to your Supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) dated and received January 12, 2018, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for INTELENCE (etravirine) 25 mg, 100 mg, 200 mg tablets.
L6: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application proposes the following changes:
L7: - To update the labeling with data to support the use of INTELENCE in HIV-1-infected pregnant women
- To add risk of adverse reactions or loss of virologic response due to drug interactions to the WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS section
- To update the DRUG INTERACTIONS section with drug-drug interaction (DDI) information for the HCV direct-acting antivirals daclatasvir, simeprevir, and elbasvir/grazoprevir and HIV protease inhibitors, darunavir/cobicistat and, atazanavir/cobicistat. In addition, DDI information for boceprevir and telaprevir was deleted as these two HCV DAAs have been withdrawn from the U.S. market
- To update the labeling in accordance with the “Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling Rule” (PLLR)
- To remove information about use of activated charcoal in the OVERDOSAGE section
- To update the CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY section with pharmacokinetic data
- To add pregnancy registry and lactation information to the PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION section
- To update the Patient Package Insert to be consistent with the Prescribing Information

L10: ## CONTENT OF LABELING
L11: As soon as possible, but no later than 14 days from the date of this letter, submit the content of labeling [21 CFR 314.50(1)] in structured product labeling (SPL) format using the FDA automated drug registration and listing system (eLIST), as described at http://www.fda.gov/ForIndustry/DataStandards/StructuredProductLabeling/default.htm. Content of labeling must be identical to the enclosed labeling (text for the package insert and text for the patient package insert), with the addition of any labeling changes in pending “Changes Being Effected” (CBE) supplements, as well as annual reportable changes not included in the enclosed labeling.
L12: Information on submitting SPL files using eList may be found in the guidance for industry titled “SPL Standard for Content of Labeling Technical Qs and As” at http://www.fda.gov/downloads/Drugs/GuidanceComplianceRegulatoryInformation/Guidances/UCM072392.pdf.
L13: The SPL will be accessible from publicly available labeling repositories.
L14: Also within 14 days, amend all pending supplemental applications that include labeling changes for this NDA, including CBE supplements for which FDA has not yet issued an action letter, with the content of labeling [21 CFR 314.50(1)(i)] in MS Word format, that includes the changes approved in this supplemental application, as well as annual reportable changes and annotate each change. To facilitate review of your submission, provide a highlighted or marked-up copy that shows all changes, as well as a clean Microsoft Word version. The marked-up copy should provide appropriate annotations, including supplement number(s) and annual report date(s).
L15: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
