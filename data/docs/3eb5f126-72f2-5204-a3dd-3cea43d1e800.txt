L15: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for &gt; 7 days and &lt; 12 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because children in this age range would experience opioid withdrawal due to iatrogenic opioid dependence, and opioids are generally not *abruptly* discontinued in children with iatrogenic opioid dependence.
L18: We are deferring submission of your pediatric studies for ages birth to 7 days (neonates), and 12 to &lt; 17 years for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric studies have not been completed.
L19: Your deferred pediatric studies required by section 505B(a) of the FDCA are required postmarketing studies. The status of these postmarketing studies must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(3)(C) of the FDCA. These required studies are listed below.

L32: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L33: Section 505(o)(3) of the FDA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L34: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDA will not be sufficient to identify an unexpected serious risk of genetic toxicity, impairment of fertility, general toxicity, or adverse effects on development in juvenile animals; it will also not be sufficient to assess a signal of serious hypertension, bradycardia, syncope, hepatotoxicity, or rebound hypertension; or identify potential unexpected serious risks of long-term use of lofexidine in the setting of gradual opioid taper.
L35: Furthermore, the new pharmacovigilance system that FDA is required to establish under section 505(k)(3) of the FDA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks
L36: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following studies:
L37: 3391-5 Conduct an *in vivo* comet assay testing lofexidine in the liver and stomach.
L38: The timetable you submitted via email on May 16, 2018, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:

Final Report Submission: 09/2018
