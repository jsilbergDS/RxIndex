L24: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L25: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L26: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for all relevant pediatric age groups for this application. A waiver was granted for pediatric patients less than one month of age on May 7, 2014.
L27: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT
L28: We have received your submission dated November 22, 2019, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the February 17, 2006 approval letter.
L29: 311-1 Deferred pediatric study under PREA for the treatment of candidemia and other forms of *Candida* infections (intra-abdominal abscess and peritonitis) in pediatric patients ages zero months to sixteen years of age.
L30: Supplement 27 provided the final study report for Protocol A8851008, “A Prospective, Open-Label Study to Assess the Pharmacokinetics, Safety &amp; Efficacy of Anidulafungin when Used to Treat Children with Invasive Candidiasis, Including Candidemia.” conducted to fulfill PMR 311-1.
L31: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above requirement was fulfilled. This completes the postmarketing requirement acknowledged in our February 17, 2006 letter.
