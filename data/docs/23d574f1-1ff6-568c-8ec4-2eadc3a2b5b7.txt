L19: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages &lt;28 days old because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because the time to make the diagnosis of dilated cardiomyopathy in newborns, and then to treat them with other drugs considered to be standard of care for that disease state, will in general take at least 28 days.
L22: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages &gt;28 days to &lt;18 years for this application because pediatric studies should be delayed until additional safety data on the effect of vericiguat on bone from preclinical juvenile toxicity studies have been collected.

L44: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following study:
L45: 4001-3 A worldwide descriptive study that collects prospective and retrospective data in women exposed to vericiguat during pregnancy to assess risk to the pregnancy and maternal complications, adverse effects on the developing fetus and neonate, and adverse effects on the infant.
