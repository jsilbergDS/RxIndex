L12: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L13: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L14: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
