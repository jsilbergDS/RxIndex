L5: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated December 31, 2019, received January 3, 2020, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Detectnet (copper Cu 64 dotatate injection).
L6: This new drug application provides for the use of Detectnet (copper Cu 64 dotatate injection) as a radioactive diagnostic agent indicated for use with positron emission tomography (PET) for localization of somatostatin receptor positive neuroendocrine tumors (NETs) in adults.
L7: ## APPROVAL &amp; LABELING
L8: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.

L16: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L17: Your application for Detectnet was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because outside expertise was not necessary; there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L18: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
