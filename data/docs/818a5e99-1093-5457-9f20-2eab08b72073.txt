L21: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L23: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ankylosing spondylitis and nonradiographic axial spondyloarthritis from 0 to less than 18 years of age for this application given that clinical studies in these populations would be highly impracticable due to low disease prevalence.
L24: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for psoriatic arthritis for ages birth to less than 6 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because of the rarity of the diagnosis of pediatric psoriatic arthritis in this age group.
L25: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for psoriatic arthritis for ages 6 to less than 18 years of age for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
L26: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) is a required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the FDCA. This required study is listed below.
