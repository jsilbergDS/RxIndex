L2: ## 1. Introduction and Discussion
L3: This review will be a brief summary of the basis for the regulatory action regarding olodaterol and the reader should review the action package for more detail. During the first review cycle, the safety and efficacy of Olodaterol was determined to be appropriate to allow for marketing. However, a Complete Response (CR) action was taken March 14, 2013 citing Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) deficiencies of the facility as the only reason precluding approval. Satisfactory resolution of these deficiencies has occurred. As such, this application should receive an Approval action.
