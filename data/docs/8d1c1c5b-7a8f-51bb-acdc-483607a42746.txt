L8: This new drug application provides for the use of PREZISTA™ (darunavir) tablets, co-administered with 100 mg of ritonavir, for the treatment of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection in antiretroviral treatment-experienced adult patients, such as those with HIV-1 strains resistant to more than one protease inhibitor.
L9: We completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved under the provisions of accelerated approval regulations (21 CFR 314.510), effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed labeling text and patient labeling. Marketing of this drug product and related activities must adhere to the substance and procedures of the referenced accelerated approval regulations.
L10: The final printed labeling (FPL) must be identical to the agreed upon enclosed labeling (text for the package insert and patient package insert). Marketing the product with FPL that is not identical to the approved labeling text may render the product misbranded and an unapproved new drug.
