L5: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated October 6, 2018, received October 9, 2018, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for ExEm Foam (air polymer-type A) intrauterine foam.
L6: We acknowledge receipt of your major amendment dated July 3, 2019, which extended the goal date by three months.
L7: This new drug application provides for the use of ExEm Foam (air polymer-type A) intrauterine foam as an ultrasound contrast agent indicated for sonohysterosalpingography to assess fallopian tube patency in women with known or suspected infertility.
L8: ## APPROVAL &amp; LABELING
L9: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.

L17: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L18: Your application for ExEm Foam was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because outside expertise was not necessary; there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable as infertility is considered an adult-related condition.
