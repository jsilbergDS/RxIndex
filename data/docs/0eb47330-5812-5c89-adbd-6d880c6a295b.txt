L3: Dear Ms. Dofiles:
L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received on April 27, 2020, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Biktarvy (bictegravir/emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide), fixed dose combination (FDC) tablets.
L5: This supplemental application proposes to update the content of the United States Prescribing Information (USPI) for Biktarvy with safety, efficacy and pharmacokinetic information obtained from the clinical trial GS-US-292-1825 entitled "A Phase 3b Open-Label Study to Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics and Efficacy of E/C/F/TAF Fixed Dose Combination (FDC) in HIV-1 Infected Subjects on Chronic Hemodialysis", with 48-week data obtained from the open-label extension portion of this trial, in which study participants were switched from Genvoya to receive Biktary.
L6: This submission also includes efficacy, PK, and safety data through 96 weeks of treatment with Genvoya to provide additional efficacy, PK, and safety data for emtricitabine and tenofovir alafenamide, both of which are components of the Biktarvy fixed-dose-combination tablet. These data were submitted to support the use of Biktarvy for the treatment of HIV-1 infection in virologically-suppressed, adult patients, with estimated creatinine clearance below 15 mL per minute who are receiving chronic hemodialysis.
