L17: ## DATING PERIOD
L18: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Sofdra (sofipironium) topical gel, shall be 36 months from the date of manufacture when stored upright at 20 to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
L19: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L20: Your application for Sofdra (sofipironium) topical gel was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because there were no issues that warranted an advisory committee discussion.
L21: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L23: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to 9 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable as axillary hyperhidrosis is unusual for those of prepubescent ages.

L32: ## COMPENDIAL STANDARDS
L33: A drug with a name recognized in the official United States Pharmacopeia or official National Formulary (USP-NF) generally must comply with the compendial standards for strength, quality, and purity, unless the difference in strength, quality, or purity is plainly stated on its label (see FD&amp;C Act § 501(b), 21 USC 351(b)). FDA typically cannot share application-specific information contained in submitted regulatory filings with third parties, which includes USP-NF. To help ensure that a drug continues to comply with compendial standards, application holders may work directly with USP-NF to revise official USP monographs. More information on the USP-NF is available on USP's website6.
