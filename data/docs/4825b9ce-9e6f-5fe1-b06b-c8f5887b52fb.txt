L7: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics application provides for a new indication for cemiplimab-rwlc, for the treatment of patients with locally advanced basal cell carcinoma (laBCC) previously treated with a hedgehog pathway inhibitor or for whom a hedgehog pathway inhibitor is not appropriate.

L22: ## **POSTMARKETING COMMITMENTS SUBJECT TO REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
## UNDER SECTION 506B**
L23: We remind you of your postmarketing commitment:
L24: 4011-1 Submit the final analysis of objective response rate and duration of response for the 84 patients with locally advanced basal cell carcinoma (laBCC) whose data was included in BLA supplement-008, in order to further characterize the durability of response in this cohort.
