L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.

Although you requested a full waiver for pediatric study requirements for the treatment of microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) or mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) metastatic colorectal cancer (CRC), we are granting a partial waiver for pediatric studies in patients less than 12 years of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable to conduct patients less than 12 years of age. The efficacy of nivolumab and nivolumab in combination with ipilimumab is extrapolated for pediatric patients 12 years of age and older and you have
L21: therefore fulfilled your obligation under PREA for the conduct of pediatric assessments for this age group.
