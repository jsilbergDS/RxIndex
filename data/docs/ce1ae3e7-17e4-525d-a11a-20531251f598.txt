L18: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: Your deferred pediatric studies required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA are required postmarketing studies. The status of these postmarketing studies must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(3)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA. These required studies are listed below.
L21: No new pediatric assessments are required because those already required for this NDA are sufficient. The following is a list of deferred required pediatric assessments which are applicable to this supplemental NDA approval for a new dosing regimen in adults with chronic idiopathic constipation:
L22: 2161-2 Conduct a safety and efficacy study in pediatric patients with chronic idiopathic constipation ages 2 to 5 years treated with Linzess (linaclotide).
L23: Final Protocol Submission	01/18
Study Completion	12/22
Final Report Submission	12/23
L24: 2161-3 Conduct a safety and efficacy study in pediatric patients with chronic Idiopathic constipation ages 6 to 17 years treated with Linzess (linaclotide).
L25: Final Protocol Submission	04/15
Study Completion	12/22
Final Report Submission	12/23
L26: Submit the protocols to your IND 063290, with a cross-reference letter to this NDA.
L27: Reports of this/these required pediatric postmarketing study(ies) must be submitted as a new drug application (NDA) or as a supplement to your approved NDA with the proposed labeling changes you believe are warranted based on the data derived from these studies. When submitting the reports, please clearly mark your submission "SUBMISSION OF REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS" in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the submission.
