L17: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE REVIEW
L18: Your application for lucinactant was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because outside expertise was not necessary; there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L19: ## EXPIRY DATING PERIOD
L20: The approved shelf life for the product is 12 months when stored under the recommended storage conditions as indicated in the label: Store SURFAXIN in a refrigerator at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F). Due to the observed instability of the drug product, we recommend that you extend the shelf life only via a prior approval supplement.
L21: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indications in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L23: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
