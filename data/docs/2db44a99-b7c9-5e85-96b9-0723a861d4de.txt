L7: ## **LICENSING**
L8: We are issuing Department of Health and Human Services U.S. License No. 2167 to Jazz Pharmaceuticals Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland, under the provisions of section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act controlling the manufacture and sale of biological products. The license authorizes you to introduce or deliver for introduction into interstate commerce, those products for which your company has demonstrated compliance with establishment and product standards.
L9: Under this license, you are authorized to manufacture the product Ziihera (zanidatamab-hrri). Ziihera is indicated for the treatment of adults with previously treated, unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC3+) biliary tract cancer as detected by an FDA approved test.

L29: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L30: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L31: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for this application because the product fails to represent a meaningful therapeutic benefit over existing therapies for pediatric patients and is unlikely to be used in a substantial number of patients in all pediatric age groups.
