Drug Product Description	
Ipsen Biopharmaceuticals, Inc. has submitted this 505(b)(1) application for IQIRVO (elafibranor) tablets, 80 mg for oral administration indicated for the treatment of primary biliary cholangitis (PBC) in combination with ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA) in adults with an inadequate response to UDCA, or as monotherapy in adults unable to tolerate UDCA. Patients are to orally take one 80 mg tablet once daily.
The drug substance elafibranor and its main active metabolite GFT1007 are dual peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor (PPAR)\alpha/\delta agonists. Elafibranor has not been previously approved or marketed in the United States and therefore, it is classified as a New Molecular Entity (NME).
IQIRVO (elafibranor) is available as round orange, approximately 8 mm, film-coated tablets, debossed
L13: with 'ELA 80' on one side. Each tablet contains 80 mg of elafibranor as the active ingredient and microcrystalline cellulose, povidone, croscarmellose sodium, (b) (4) colloidal silica, and magnesium stearate as inactive ingredients. The film coating consists of polyvinyl alcohol (b) (4) titanium dioxide (b) (4) talc, iron oxide yellow and iron oxide red. All inactive ingredients including the tablet coating components are compendial materials. The quantity of each inactive ingredient is at or below the limit listed in the Agency's IID for approved oral drug products.
IQIRVO tablets will be packaged as 30-count tablets in high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle with a white child-resistant (b) (4) cap (b) (4) (b) (4) (b) (4) This drug product should be stored at 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) with an expiration dating period of 24 months.
