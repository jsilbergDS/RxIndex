L15: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
L18: ## POSTMARKETING COMMITMENTS SUBJECT TO THE REPORTING REQUIREMENTS UNDER SECTION 506B
L19: We remind you of your postmarketing commitments:
L20: 4082-1 Improve the drug tolerance of the current anti-ravulizumab-cwvz antibody screening and confirmatory assay or develop a new assay to improve the drug tolerance. The assay should be capable of detecting binding anti-ravulizumab-cwvz antibodies (ADA) in the presence of ravulizumab-cwvz levels expected to be present in serum at the time of patient sampling. The final report should include development and validation data to support use of the assay in pediatric patients with paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH).
