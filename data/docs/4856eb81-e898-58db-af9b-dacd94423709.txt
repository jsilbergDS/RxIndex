L21: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L23: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable to conduct due to the rarity of the condition in children.
