L6: ## LICENSING
L7: We have approved your BLA for Sogroya (somapacitan-beco) effective this date. L34: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L35: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L36: We are deferring submission of your pediatric studies because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric studies have not been completed.
L37: Your deferred pediatric studies required under section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act are required postmarketing studies. The status of these postmarketing studies must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 601.28 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.
L38: These required studies are listed below.
L39: 3890-1 Complete ongoing study NN8640-4172, a 1-year phase 2, randomized, open-label, active-control, dose-finding trial, to investigate efficacy and safety of once-weekly somapacitan versus daily Norditropin (somatropin) in pre-pubertal children with Growth Hormone Deficiency (GHD) (boys \ge 2.5 and \le 10 years of age, and girls \ge 2.5 and \le 9 years of age) followed by a 2-year single-arm period to evaluate safety in this cohort, and a 4-year single-arm period that also enrolls cohorts of younger children with GHD (boys and girls &lt; 2.5 years of age) and older children with GHD (boys &gt; 10 and \le 17 years of age, and girls &gt; 9 and \le 17 years of age) to evaluate safety in all three cohorts.
L40: Study Completion: October 2024
Final Report Submission: March 2025
L41: 3890-2 Complete ongoing study NN8640-4263, a 1-year phase 3 randomized, parallel group, open-label trial, to evaluate the efficacy and safety of once weekly somapacitan versus daily Norditropin (somatropin) in pre-pubertal pediatric patients (boys \ge 2.5 and \le 10 years of age, and girls \ge 2.5 and \le 9 years of age) with GHD, followed by a 3-year single-arm extension period to evaluate long-term safety.
L42: Study Completion: December 2024
Final Report Submission: May 2025
L43: Submit protocol amendments to your IND 116327 with a cross-reference letter to this BLA. Reports of these required pediatric postmarketing studies must be submitted as a biologics license application (BLA) or as a supplement to your approved BLA with the proposed labeling changes you believe are warranted based on the data derived from these studies. When submitting the reports, please clearly mark your submission **"SUBMISSION OF REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS"** in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the submission.
