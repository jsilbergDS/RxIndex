L30: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L31: Section 505(o)(3) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L32: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess the potential risks of benznidazole metabolites and the potential for male infertility.
L33: Furthermore, the new pharmacovigilance system that FDA is required to establish under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks.
L34: Finally, we have determined that a pharmacokinetic study (rather than an observational study) and a nonclinical study will be sufficient to assess these potential risks.
L35: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct and submit the following:
L36: **PMR 3247-2:** Conduct a human absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME)/mass balance study to evaluate the routes and rates of benznidazole excretion, ascertain whether benznidazole has circulating drug metabolites, and if identified evaluate the routes and rates of excretion for benznidazole metabolites.
L37: The timetable you submitted on August 17, 2017, states that you will conduct the study according to the following schedule:
L38: Draft protocol submission:	10/2017
Final protocol submission:	11/2017
Study completion:	04/2018
Final report submission:	08/2018
L39: **PMR 3247-3:** Submit the results of Study AB21206 entitled Benznidazole - Fertility toxicity study by the oral route (twice daily gavage) in the male rat (Segment I) followed by a 13-week treatment-free period.
L40: The timetable you submitted on August 16, 2017, states that you will follow the schedule below:
L41: Study completion:	Completed
Final report submission:	09/2017
L42: Submit clinical protocol(s) to your PIND 118976 with a cross-reference letter to this NDA. L44: FDA will consider the submission of your annual report under section 506B and 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii) to satisfy the periodic reporting requirement under section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) provided that you include the elements listed in 505(o) and 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii).
