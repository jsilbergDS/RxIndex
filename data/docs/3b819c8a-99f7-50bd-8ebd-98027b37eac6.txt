L16: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L17: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L18: Your deferred pediatric studies required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act are required postmarketing studies. The status of these postmarketing studies must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(3)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. These required studies are listed below.
L19: 3505-1 Conduct a randomized, placebo-controlled study to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of AEMCOLO (rifamycin) for the treatment of travelers' diarrhea in children from 6 to 11 years of age.
L20: The timetable you submitted on November 15, 2018, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:

Study/Trial Completion:	06/2022
Final Report Submission:	12/2022
L22: 3505-2 Conduct a randomized, placebo-controlled study to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of AEMCOLO (rifamycin) for the treatment of travelers' diarrhea in children from 12 to 17 years of age.
L23: The timetable you submitted on November 15, 2018, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L24: Draft Protocol Submission:	Submitted
Final Protocol Submission:	03/2019

When submitting the reports, please clearly mark your submission "SUBMISSION OF REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS" in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the submission.
