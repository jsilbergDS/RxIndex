L5: Please refer to your Supplemental Biologics License Applications (sBLAs), Supplement 017 dated June 22, 2017 and received June 23, 2017 and Supplement 018 dated and received August 11, 2017, and your amendments submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for GAZYVA® (obinutuzumab) injection, 1000 mg/40 mL (25 mg/mL).
L6: ## **Supplement 017**
L7: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics application provides for a new indication for GAZYVA in combination with chemotherapy, followed by GAZYVA monotherapy in patients achieving at least a partial remission, for the treatment of adult patients with previously untreated stage II bulky, III or IV follicular lymphoma.
L8: The new indication is based on Study BO21223 (GALLIUM), which evaluated chemotherapy combined with either obinutuzumab or rituximab, followed in responding patients by obinutuzumab or rituximab monotherapy, in patients with previously untreated non-Hodgkin lymphoma.
L9: ## **Supplement 018**
L10: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics application provides for updates to the United States Prescribing Information (USPI), under Contraindication and Warnings and Precautions sections with the addition of hypersensitivity reactions including serum sickness. This information is based on Global Safety database and available literature data.
