L7: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA), dated and received November 30, 2024, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Enhertu (fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki) 100 mg lyophilized powder for solution for intravenous injection.
L8: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics license application provides for the use of Enhertu for the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC 3+) solid tumors who have received prior systemic treatment and have no satisfactory alternative treatment options.
L9: This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on tumor response rate and duration of response. Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon verification and description of clinical benefit in a confirmatory trial.

L40: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L41: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L42: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for ages 0 to &lt;1 month old because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable due to the rarity of pediatric patients in this age group who would be eligible for investigational treatment with Enhertu.
L43: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages 1 month to &lt; 17 years old for this application because additional pediatric nonclinical data are needed to inform the design of the pediatric clinical study.
L44: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act are required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 601.28 and section 505B(a)(4)(B) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. This required study is listed below.
