L18: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable.
L21: - For the indication to *reduce the risk of sustained eGFR decline, end-stage kidney disease, and cardiovascular death in adults with chronic kidney disease at risk of progression*. There is not a good understanding of the mechanism(s) of dapagliflozin's clinical benefits in adults with chronic kidney disease (CKD). Given the differences in etiologies of CKD in adults and children, one cannot assume the benefits observed in adults will translate to pediatric patients, and, to date, a suitable biomarker to bridge the findings in adults to children has not been identified. In adults with non-diabetic causes of CKD enrolled in the DAPA-CKD trial, the treatment effect of dapagliflozin on urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) was small and accounted for a minor proportion of the effect on clinical outcomes. Absent compelling data to support a biomarker-based approach, it is not clear it is feasible to conduct a clinical study in pediatric patients with CKD at present.
