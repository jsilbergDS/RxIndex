L4: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA) received February 26, 2025, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for OPDIVO (nivolumab), injection.
L5: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics license application updates the OPDIVO (nivolumab) Prescribing Information (PI) and Medication Guide (MG) revising the following indications to reflect the population (i.e., PD-L1 \ge 1) with a favorable risk-benefit assessment:
L6: - OPDIVO, in combination with fluoropyrimidine- and platinum-containing chemotherapy, is indicated for the first-line treatment of adult patients with unresectable advanced or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) whose tumors express PD-L1 (\ge 1).
- OPDIVO, in combination with ipilimumab, is indicated for the first-line treatment of adult patients with unresectable advanced or metastatic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) whose tumors express PD-L1 (\ge 1).
- OPDIVO, in combination with fluoropyrimidine- and platinum-containing chemotherapy, is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with advanced or metastatic gastric cancer, gastroesophageal junction cancer, and esophageal adenocarcinoma whose tumors express PD-L1 (\ge 1).
