L8: - Cerebral arteriography, including intra-arterial digital subtraction angiography (IA-DSA), in adults and pediatric patients

- Radiographic evaluation of cardiac chambers and related arteries in pediatric patients
L9: ## Intravenous Procedures
L10: - Computed tomography (CT) of the head and body in adults and pediatric patients
- CT angiography of intracranial, visceral, and lower extremity arteries in adults and pediatric patients
- Coronary CT angiography in adults and pediatric patients
- CT urography in adults and pediatric patients

L18: ## DATING PERIOD
L19: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for lomervu (iomeprol) injection shall be 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 25°C/60%RH in the carton protected from light.
