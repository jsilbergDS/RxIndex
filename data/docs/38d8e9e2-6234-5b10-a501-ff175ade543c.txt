L7: This new drug application provides for the use of Nourianz (istradefylline) tablets as adjunctive treatment to levodopa/carbidopa in adult patients with Parkinson's disease experiencing "off" episodes.

L17: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L18: Your application for Nourianz was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because the safety profile of Nourianz is similar to that of other drugs approved for the proposed indication, and there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable, as Parkinson's disease is very rare in the pediatric population, and clinical studies are not practical.
