L35: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L36: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L37: Because none of these criteria apply to these supplemental applications, you are exempt from this requirement.
