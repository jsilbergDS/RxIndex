L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received September 22, 2021, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act for Mekinist (trametinib) tablets.
L5: We acknowledge receipt of your March 14, 2022, major amendment which extended the goal date by three months.
L6: This supplemental application provides for the following new indication:
L7: *MEKINIST in combination with dabrafenib is indicated for the treatment of adult and pediatric patients 6 years of age and older with unresectable or metastatic solid tumors with BRAF V600E mutation who have progressed following prior treatment and have no satisfactory alternative treatment options. This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on overall response rate and duration of response. Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon verification and description of clinical benefit in a confirmatory trial(s).*
L8: *This supplemental application also includes the following new limitation of use:*
L9: *MEKINIST is not indicated for treatment of patients with colorectal cancer because of known intrinsic resistance to BRAF inhibition.*

L28: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L29: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L30: We are deferring submission of your pediatric studies in pediatric patients one to six years of age until October 2022, because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and pediatric patients 6 years of age and older, and the pediatric studies have not been completed.
L31: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA is a required postmarketing study. The status of these postmarketing studies must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA. These required studies are listed below.
