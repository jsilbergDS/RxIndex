L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric studies requirement for ages 13 to 17 years for this application.
L22: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT(S)/COMMITMENT(S)
L23: We have received your submissions dated July 9, 2014, and July 29, 2016 containing the final reports for the following postmarketing requirements listed in the October 28, 2010 approval letter.
L24: 1701-1 A deferred pediatric study under PREA for the treatment of schizophrenia in pediatric patients ages 13-17 years. A study to obtain pharmacokinetic data and provide information pertinent to dosing of lurasidone tablets in the relevant pediatric population.
L25: 1701-2 A deferred pediatric study under PREA for the treatment of schizophrenia in pediatric patients ages 13-17 years. A study of the efficacy and safety of lurasidone tablets in the relevant pediatric population.
L26: We have reviewed your submissions and conclude that the above requirements were fulfilled.
L27: This completes all of your postmarketing requirements and postmarketing commitments acknowledged in our October 28, 2010, letter.
