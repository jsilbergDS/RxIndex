L6: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for the following changes to the MAVYRET™ US Prescribing Information (USPI):
L7: - Addition of safety and efficacy data for subjects with genotype (GT) 5 and 6 Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) infection from the ENDURANCE-5,6 study
- Contraindicate the use of MAVYRET™ in patients with moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B) or those with any history of prior hepatic decompensation
- Update WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS with new subsection 5.2, Risk of Hepatic Decompensation/Failure in Patients with Evidence of Advanced Liver Disease
- Update ADVERSE REACTIONS with subsection, 6.2, Post Marketing Experience
- Update DRUG INTERACTIONS, Established and Other Potential Drug Interactions subsection, with information regarding INR fluctuations and dysglycemia
- Update DRUG INTERACTIONS with subsection 7.4, Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) for Opioid Use Disorder, to highlight drug interaction information
- To make corresponding changes to the Patient Information
