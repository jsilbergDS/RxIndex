L17: ## **FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT**
L18: We have received your submission dated July 30, 2019, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the post approval new postmarketing requirement letter for BLA 125370 dated September 19, 2011.
L19: 2661-14 Conduct a one-year, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial with Benlysta (belimumab) in 5,000 patients with active, autoantibody-positive systemic lupus erythematosus to evaluate Benlysta's long term safety profile including adverse events of special interest (e.g., mortality, malignancy, serious and opportunistic infections and depression (suicidality)).
L20: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above requirement was fulfilled.
L21: We remind you that there are postmarketing requirements listed in the March 9, 2011, approval letter that are still open.
