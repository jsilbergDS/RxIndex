L0: The FDA approved Saphris (asenapine) sublingual tablets for the treatment of schizophrenia and bipolar I disorder in adults. This regulatory action follows a complete response to a previous action letter, confirming the drug's safety and efficacy for the specified indications. The approval includes requirements for postmarketing studies, including deferred pediatric assessments and further characterization of dosing and metabolic effects. The sponsor must submit final labeling and container materials, and adhere to ongoing reporting requirements for postmarketing commitments. This decision marks the formal authorization for the marketing and distribution of asenapine in the United States.
