L6: Please refer to your Supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) dated March 24, 2017, received March 24, 2017, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Daliresp (roflumilast) 250 mcg, and 500 mcg Tablets.
L7: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for labeling changes to incorporate results of a post marketing study (Study RO-2455-302-RD [OPTIMIZE]), which evaluated dose up-titration as a strategy to improve roflumilast tolerability and thereby reduce treatment discontinuation. This supplement also provides for the introduction of a roflumilast 250 mcg tablet to support a starting dose of 250 mcg once daily for the first 4 weeks followed by an increase to the roflumilast maintenance dosage of 500 mcg once daily.

L18: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This indication (treatment to reduce the risk of COPD exacerbations in patients with severe COPD associated with chronic bronchitis and a history of exacerbations.) does not occur in children; therefore, pediatric studies are not required.
