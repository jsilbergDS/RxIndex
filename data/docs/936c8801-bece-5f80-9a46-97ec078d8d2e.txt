L6: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated April 22, 2025, received April 22, 2025, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Elucirem (gadopiclenol) injection.
L7: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application updates the U.S. Prescribing Information to include an extension of the existing Elucirem indications to pediatric patients &lt; 2 years of age, including term neonates. In addition, this supplement provides for revisions to subsection 8.1 Pregnancy to reflect clinical experience regarding the risk of in utero exposure to gadolinium-based contrast agents (GBCAs) based on a review of scientific literature.

L16: REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L17: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L18: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for patients &lt; 2 years of age (including term neonates) for this application.
L19: FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT
L20: We have received your submission dated April 22, 2025, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the September 21, 2022, approval letter.
L21: 4341-1 Conduct a study of approximately 40 neonates and infants aged &lt; 2 years that will receive a single dose of 0.05 mmol/kg gadopiclenol intravenously for the evaluation of the plasma pharmacokinetics profile of gadopiclenol (POP-PK analysis).
