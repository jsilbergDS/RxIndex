L23: POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)
L24: Section 505(o)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L25: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a signal of serious risk of adverse reactions caused by acetylcholinesterase inhibition or by other mechanisms.
L26: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L27: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to assess a signal of serious risk of adverse reactions caused by acetylcholinesterase inhibition or by other mechanisms.
L28: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following trial:
L29: 4939-1 Conduct an open-label, single-arm, prospective trial for the treatment of uncomplicated gonorrhea to evaluate the safety of Blujepa (gepotidacin).
