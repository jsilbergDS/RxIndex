L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because mCRC is extremely rare in the pediatric population and the necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable to conduct in pediatric patients.
