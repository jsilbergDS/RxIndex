L61: A number of inspectional observations were noted during the clinical investigator inspections. At Dr. Muntoni's site, there was potential unblinding of treatment assignment in two subjects. L62: Other inspectional observations at Dr. Muntoni's site included obtaining muscle biopsy samples after Week 48 for all five enrolled subjects and under-dosing (10-20%) of investigational product in three of five subjects during the initial weeks of the blinded Part 1 of Protocol 4053-101. L66: ## II. BACKGROUND
L67: Golodirsen injection is being developed under NDA 211970 (IND 119982) for the treatment of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) in patients who have a confirmed mutation of the DMD gene that is amenable to exon 53 skipping. The sponsor has submitted a Phase 1/2 ongoing clinical study, Protocol 4053-101, to support the accelerated approval of golodirsen for this indication. Accelerated approval is based on a biological primary endpoint, change in dystrophin expression from baseline to Week 48. An ongoing Phase 3 study, Protocol 4045-301, is the confirmatory clinical study to support accelerated approval. However, in this NDA submission are only interim safety data but not efficacy data from Protocol 4045-301.
L68: ## Protocol 4053-101
L69: *Title:* “A 2-part, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-titration, safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics study (Part 1) followed by an open-label efficacy and safety evaluation (Part 2) of SRP-4053 [golodirsen] in patients with Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy amenable to Exon 53 skipping”
L70: *Subjects:* 39
L71: *Sites:* 5 sites; England (2), France (1), Italy (1), and U.S. (1)
L72: *Study Initiation and Completion Dates:* 1/13/2015 - ongoing
L73: *Database Cut-Off Date:* 6/29/2018
L74: This is an ongoing, first-in-human, multiple-dose study in subjects with genotypically confirmed DMD with a deletion amenable to exon 53 skipping. Included were males, 6 to 15 years of age (inclusive), with intact right and left biceps muscles or an alternative upper arm muscle group, who were able to achieve a Six-Minute Walk Test (6MWT) distance of \ge 250 meters at screening and baseline (with a baseline mean 6MWT within 15% of screening mean 6MWT) and a North Star Ambulatory Assessment (NSAA) total score &gt;17 or Rise (Gowers') time &lt;7 seconds, and who have been on a stable dose of oral corticosteroids for at least 24 weeks prior to randomization.
L75: The study was conducted in two parts. Part 1 was a double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose titration to assess the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics of golodirsen over approximately 12 weeks. Following a 4 to 6-week screening period, twelve subjects were randomized (2:1) to the following:
L76: - Golodirsen IV infusion in four escalating dose levels (4, 10, 20, and 30 mg/kg) administered once weekly with at least 2 weeks at each dose level
- Placebo IV infusion administered once weekly
L77: Part 2 of the study is a 168-week ongoing open-label evaluation to assess the efficacy and safety of golodirsen. All 12 subjects from Part 1 continued into Part 2, with an additional enrollment of approximately 12 new subjects with a clinical diagnosis of DMD with a deletion amenable to exon 53 skipping. Subjects received open-label golodirsen 30 mg/kg (or the maximum tolerated dose) IV infusion once weekly for 168 weeks.
L78: Additionally, up to 24 subjects not receiving treatment (untreated) were enrolled in Part 2. These untreated subjects had a clinical diagnosis of DMD with a confirmed deletion not amenable to treatment by exon 53 skipping. These subjects did not receive golodirsen but received the current standard of care.
L79: Muscle biopsies were performed at baseline in Part 1, baseline in Part 2 (for new subjects only), and at Week 48 in Part 2 (all treated subjects). Untreated subjects did not undergo muscle biopsies. Clinical assessments included the 6MWT, NSAA, pulmonary function tests, and other assessments as outlined in the protocol.
L80: There were two *primary efficacy endpoints* for this study:
L81: - Change from baseline in dystrophin expression in golodirsen-treated subjects at Week 48 (biological endpoint)
- Change from baseline on the 6MWT in treated and untreated subjects at Week 144 (clinical functional endpoint)
L82: Accelerated approval is based on the primary biological endpoint, dystrophin expression at Week 48 compared to baseline. The clinical efficacy endpoint, change from baseline in 6MWT, will be analyzed at Week 144 in this ongoing study. The sponsor has submitted interim 6MWT results for 16 subjects who reached the Week 144 time point by the NDA data cut-off date of 6/29/2018.
L83: ## Protocol 4045-301
L84: *Title:* “A double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter study with an open-label extension to evaluate the efficacy and safety of SRP-4045 [casimersen] and SRP-4053 [golodirsen] in patients with Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy”
L85: *Subjects:* Actively enrolling, data for 50 subjects included in NDA submission
L86: *Sites:* 28 sites; United States (15) England (3), France (1), Italy (2), Belgium (1), Sweden (1), Germany (1), Spain (3), and Czech Republic (1)
L87: *Study Initiation and Completion Dates:* This study is ongoing
L88: *Database Cut-Off Date:* 6/29/2018
L89: This is an ongoing, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of two investigational drugs, casimersen and golodirsen, in subjects with genetically confirmed DMD with deletion mutations amenable to exon 45 and 53 skipping. Inclusion criteria were similar to Protocol 4053-101 but differed in the inclusion also of subjects with DMD amenable to exon 45
L90: skipping, age 7 to 13 years of age (inclusive), and able to achieve a 6MWT distance of \ge 300 and \le 450 meters at screening and baseline.
L91: The study included a screening period (up to 8 weeks), a 96-week double-blind period, and an up to 48-week open label period. Following the screening period, eligible subjects with DMD amenable to exon 45 skipping are randomized (2:1) to casimersen 30 mg/kg/week or placebo; eligible subjects with DMD amenable to exon 53 skipping are randomized (2:1) to golodirsen 30 mg/kg/week or placebo. Study drug is administered as weekly IV infusions. Randomization is stratified by genotype and age.
L92: The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the effect of casimersen and golodirsen compared to placebo on ambulation, endurance, and muscle function, as measured by the 6MWT. A secondary objective is to evaluate the effect of study drug on dystrophin protein expression in biopsied muscle.
L93: ## Rationale for Site Selection
L94: The clinical sites were chosen primarily based on numbers of enrolled subjects. The sponsor, Sarepta, was chosen since there is no PDUFA inspectional history.

L244: In summary, whole cell patch clamp studies were performed on hERG channels expressed in recombinant cells.
