L4: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated December 18, 2018, received December 18, 2018, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Piqray® (alpelisib), 50 mg, 150 mg, and 200 mg Tablets.
L5: This new drug application provides for the use of Piqray® (alpelisib), 50 mg, 150 mg, and 200 mg Tablets in combination with fulvestrant for the treatment of postmenopausal women, and men, with hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, PIK3CA-mutated, advanced or metastatic breast cancer as detected by an FDA-approved test following progression on or after an endocrine-based regimen.
L6: ## APPROVAL &amp; LABELING
L7: We have completed our review of this application. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.

L15: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L16: Your application for Piqray® was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because the application did not raise significant safety or efficacy issues that were unexpected in the intended population. Outside expertise was not necessary; there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L17: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable since the disease does not occur in children.
