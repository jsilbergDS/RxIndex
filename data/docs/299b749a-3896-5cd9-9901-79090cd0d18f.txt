L15: ## DATING PERIOD
L16: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Zelsuvmi (berdazimer) topical gel shall be 28 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 2°C - 8°C.
L17: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L18: Your application for Zelsuvmi was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because the application did not raise significant public health questions on the role of the drug in the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of a disease.
L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to 1 year of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable.

L31: ## **COMPENDIAL STANDARDS**
L32: A drug with a name recognized in the official United States Pharmacopeia or official National Formulary (USP-NF) generally must comply with the compendial standards for strength, quality, and purity, unless the difference in strength, quality, or purity is plainly stated on its label (see FD&amp;C Act § 501(b), 21 USC 351(b)). FDA typically cannot share application-specific information contained in submitted regulatory filings with third parties, which includes USP-NF. To help ensure that a drug continues to comply with compendial standards, application holders may work directly with USP-NF to revise official USP monographs. More information on the USP-NF is available on USP's website6.
