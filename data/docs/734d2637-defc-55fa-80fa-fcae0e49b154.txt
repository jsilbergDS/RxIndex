L34: ## 5.1 Contamination of Tip and Solution
L35: To minimize contaminating the dropper tip and solution, care should be taken not to touch the eyelids or surrounding areas with the dropper tip of the bottle. Keep bottle tightly closed when not in use.
L36: There are no adequate and well-controlled studies of bepotastine besilate in pregnant women.
L37: ## 5.2 Contact Lens Use
L38: Patients should be advised not to wear a contact lens if their eye is red. **Bepreve™** should not be used to treat contact lens-related irritation.
L39: Because animal reproduction studies are not always predictive of human response, **Bepreve™** (bepotastine besilate ophthalmic solution) 1.5% should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.
L40: **Bepreve™** should not be instilled while wearing contact lenses. Remove contact lenses prior to instillation of **Bepreve™**. The preservative in **Bepreve™**, benzalkonium chloride, may be absorbed by soft contact lenses. Lenses may be reinserted after 10 minutes following administration of **Bepreve™**.
L41: ## 8.3 Nursing Mothers
L42: Following a single 3 mg/kg oral dose of radio-labeled bepotastine besilate to nursing rats 11 days after delivery, the maximum concentration of radioactivity in milk was 0.40 µg eq/mL 1 hour after administration; at 48 hours after administration the concentration was below detection limits. The milk concentration was higher than the maternal blood plasma concentration at each time of measurement.
L43: ## 5.3 Topical Ophthalmic Use Only
L44: **Bepreve™** is for topical ophthalmic use only.

L54: ## 12.3 Pharmacokinetics
L55: **Absorption:** The extent of systemic exposure to bepatastine following topical ophthalmic administration of bepatastine besilate 1% and 1.5% ophthalmic solutions was evaluated in 12 healthy adults. Following one drop of 1% or 1.5% bepatastine besilate ophthalmic solution to both eyes four time daily (QID) for seven days, bepatastine plasma concentrations peaked at approximately one to two hours post-instillation. Maximum plasma concentration for the 1% and 1.5% strengths were 5.1 \pm 2.5 ng/mL and 7.3 \pm 1.9 ng/mL, respectively. Plasma concentration at 24 hours post-instillation were the below quantifiable limit (2ng/mL) in 11/12 subjects in the two dose groups.
L56: ## 8.5 Geriatric Use
L57: No overall difference in safety or effectiveness has been observed between elderly and younger patients.

L61: **Metabolism:** *In vitro* metabolism studies with human liver microsomes demonstrated that bepatastine is minimally metabolized by CYP450 isozymes.
