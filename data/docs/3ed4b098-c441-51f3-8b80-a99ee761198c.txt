L8: Description: The drug product, LCZ696 (sacubitril/valsartan) 50 mg, 100 mg, 200 mg film-coated tablet is an immediate release dosage form for oral administration. The 100mg dosage form is a pale yellow, ovaloid, biconvex film-coated tablet with beveled edges, unscored, debossed with “NVR” on one side and “L1” on the other side. The 200mg dosage form is a light pink, ovaloid, biconvex film-coated tablet with beveled edges, unscored, debossed with “NVR” on one side and “L11” on the other side. All three dosage forms will be produced at the Novartis Stein Switzerland Facility.
