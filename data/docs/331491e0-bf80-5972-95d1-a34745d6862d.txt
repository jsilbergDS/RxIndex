L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received August 14, 2023, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for RETEVMO (selpercatinib) capsules.
L5: This Prior Approval sNDA provides for conversion to traditional approval of Retevmo capsules for the treatment of adult and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with advanced or metastatic thyroid cancer with a *RET* gene fusion, as detected by an FDA-approved test, who require systemic therapy and who are radioactive iodine-refractory (if radioactive iodine is appropriate).
