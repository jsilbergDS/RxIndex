L17: ## SUBPART E NOT FULFILLED
L18: We approved this BLA under the regulations at 21 CFR 601 Subpart E for Accelerated Approval of Biological Products for Serious or Life-Threatening Illnesses. We acknowledge the February 27, 2015, cover letter to this supplement, which stated that “this supplement to the BLA is being submitted to fulfill the Post-Marketing Requirement 2838-1 outlined in the 22-Dec-2014 accelerated approval letter for BLA 125554,” reproduced below.
L19: 2838-1 Conduct and submit the results of a multicenter, randomized trial or trials establishing the superiority of nivolumab over standard therapy in adult patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma who are refractory to ipilimumab or who have not been previously treated with ipilimumab.
L20: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the terms of the requirement were not met since this supplement has verified clinical benefit only for the treatment of patients with BRAF wild-type melanoma, which is a subset of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma described in PMR 2838-1. You must submit a final report that demonstrates you have met the terms of the above-listed requirement by December 31, 2016.
L21: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L23: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
