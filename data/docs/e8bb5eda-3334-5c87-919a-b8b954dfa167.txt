L9: *YERVOY in combination with nivolumab, is indicated for the treatment of unresectable or metastatic melanoma in adult and pediatric patients 12 years and older.*
L10: Editorial changes associated with the indication were made to INDICATIONS AND USAGE, Unresectable or Metastatic Melanoma subsection; USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS, Pediatric Use subsection; CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY, Pharmacokinetics subsection, and associated editorial changes to HIGHLIGHTS and the Medication Guide.

L20: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L21: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L22: Because the biological product for this indication has orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
