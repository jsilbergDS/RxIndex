L15: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: PREA assessments were previously waived under the original NDA submission. This Prior Approval Supplement triggered PREA for a new dosage regimen. This submission contained pediatric data that fulfilled a pediatric assessment in children ages 1 year to &lt;18 years with dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM). We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages one month to &lt;1 year because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable because of rarity of DCM in this age group.
L18: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to one month because there is evidence strongly suggesting that the drug product would be ineffective and unsafe in this pediatric group because of potential impact of renin-angiotensin system blockade on kidney development.
