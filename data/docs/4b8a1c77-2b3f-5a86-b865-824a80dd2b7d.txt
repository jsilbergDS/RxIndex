L17: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We are waiving the pediatric study(ies) requirement for this application because NSCLC is extremely rare in pediatric patients, and it would be highly impracticable and nearly impossible to conduct studies in pediatric patients.

L28: 4349-2 Conduct an integrated analysis containing data from ongoing or planned clinical trials and other data sources such as post-marketing reports, real-world evidence and other sources to further characterize the safety and efficacy of cemiplimab-rwlc in combination with platinum-doublet chemotherapy in older adults (e.g., ages 75 years and older), females, and racial and ethnic minority patients that is reflective of the U.S. population of patients with NSCLC.
