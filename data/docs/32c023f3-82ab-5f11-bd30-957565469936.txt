L18: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
L21: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L22: Section 505(o)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L23: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess the signal of a serious risk of decreased effectiveness of oral contraceptives (OC) due to a drug-drug interaction between nintedanib and OC. If coadministration of nintedanib resulted in a lower OC exposure, it will lead to compromised OC efficacy.

L25: Furthermore, the new pharmacovigilance system that FDA is required to establish under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L26: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following:
L27: 3706-1 A drug-drug interaction trial to assess the pharmacokinetics, safety, and tolerability for the coadministration of a combined oral contraceptive (containing ethinyl estradiol and levonorgestrel) with Ofev 150 mg twice daily.
