L3: Dear David Craig Zecher II:
L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug applications (sNDAs) dated and received December 12, 2023, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) and pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Farxiga (dapagliflozin) tablets and Xigduo XR (dapagliflozin and metformin hydrochloride) extended-release tablets, respectively.
L5: These Prior Approval sNDAs provide for the expansion of the approved indication as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus to include pediatric patients aged 10 years and older, with associated revisions to the Farxiga and Xigduo XR Prescribing Information and Medication Guides based on the results of the pediatric study D1680C00019 (CV181375) entitled, A 26-week, multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, parallel group, phase 3 trial with a 26-week safety extension period evaluating the safety and efficacy of dapagliflozin 5 and 10 mg, and saxagliptin 2.5 and 5 mg in pediatric patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus who are between 10 and below 18 years of age.
L6: This study was conducted to address the following postmarketing requirement (PMR) established in the April 24, 2017, Acknowledge New Postmarketing Requirement letter:
L7: 3199-1 Conduct a 26-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the monotherapies saxagliptin and dapagliflozin for the treatment of pediatric patients ages 10 to &lt; 18 years with type 2 diabetes mellitus, followed by a 26-week site- and subject-blinded safety extension period (weeks 26 to 52). Background therapy will consist of either metformin, insulin, or metformin plus insulin. A second randomization will take place at week 14, with uptitration of dose (saxagliptin may be increased from 2.5 mg to 5 mg; dapagliflozin from 5 mg to 10 mg) for approximately half of the subjects with a hemoglobin A1C greater than or equal to 7%.
