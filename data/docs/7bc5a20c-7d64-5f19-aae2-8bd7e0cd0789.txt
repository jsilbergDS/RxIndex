L22: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Brenzavvy (bexagliflozin) tablets shall be 60 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 20°C to 25°C.
L23: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L24: Your application for Brenzavvy was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because this drug is not the first in its class and it did not raise efficacy or safety issues requiring external input.
L25: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L26: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L27: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to 9 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because there are too few children in this age range with type 2 diabetes mellitus to study.
L28: 2 We update guidances periodically. For the most recent version of a guidance, check the FDA Guidance Documents Database https://www.fda.gov/RegulatoryInformation/Guidances/default.htm.
L29: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages 10 to 17 years (inclusive) for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
L30: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the FDCA is a required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the FDCA. This required study is listed below.
L31: 4296-1 Conduct a 24-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel group study of the safety, efficacy, pharmacokinetics (PK), and pharmacodynamics (PD) of bexagliflozin for the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus in pediatric patients ages 10 to 17 years (inclusive), followed by a 28-week double-blind, controlled extension. The bexagliflozin dose will be determined based on the initial PK/PD lead-in portion of the study.
