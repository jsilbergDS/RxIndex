L23: ## RISK EVALUATION AND MITIGATION STRATEGY (REMS) REQUIREMENTS
L24: The REMS for GATTEX (teduglutide) was originally approved on December 21, 2012, and the most recent REMS modification was approved on December 18, 2018. The REMS consists of elements to assure safe use and a timetable for submission of assessments of the REMS. Your proposed modifications to the REMS consist of changes to align with this efficacy supplement's approval.
L25: In accordance with section 505-1 of the FD&amp;C Act, we have determined that the following REMS modifications are necessary to ensure the benefits of the drug outweigh the risks:
L26: - Incorporating information about the new population, which expands usage to include pediatric patients 1 year of age and older with SBS who are dependent on parenteral support, into the existing REMS.
- Making formatting and editorial changes, typographical corrections, and administrative changes throughout the REMS document and all REMS materials.
L27: Your proposed modified REMS, submitted and received on May 10, 2019 and appended to this letter, is approved. The modified REMS consists of elements to assure safe use and a timetable for submission of assessments of the REMS.
L28: The timetable for submission of assessments of the REMS remains the same as that approved on December 21, 2012. Changes to the assessment plan are included in this modification.
L29: ## **Revised Assessment Plan**
L30: The effectiveness of the GATTEX REMS will be evaluated through healthcare professional training metrics and knowledge, attitude, and behavior (KAB) surveys of GATTEX prescribers, patients, and caregivers' understanding of the potential risks associated with GATTEX. The REMS assessment will include an evaluation of the effectiveness of the REMS and recommendations on any areas for program improvements or modifications, as appropriate. The frequency of the KAB surveys changed from annually to every two years at the FDA's request on May 20, 2015.
L31: - Surveys will be conducted with a random sample of prescribers, patients, and caregivers, identified through dispensing data from specialty pharmacies, in order to assess their
L32: awareness and understanding of the potential risks associated with GATTEX. Assessment protocols and survey instruments will be submitted to the FDA at least 90 days before the assessments are conducted.
L33: - Protocols will include the rationale for and description of methodologies and questionnaires to be used to evaluate prescribers', patients', and caregivers' understanding about the safe use of GATTEX. Shire-NPS Pharmaceuticals, Inc. will initiate the prescriber, patient, and caregivers' KAB surveys so that survey results can be included in the REMS Assessment Report to be submitted to FDA 12 months after REMS approval and annually thereafter. The protocol will include:Expected sample size and confidence intervals associated with that sample size
- Description of the methodology for recruitment and selection of the prescribers' sample
- Selection criteria for inclusion into the survey
- Description of how and when the survey will be administered
- Explanation of the design features and controls that will be included to minimize bias and compensate for any limitations in the methodology
L34: The proposed REMS assessment plan includes the following:
L35: - Communication Activities:Date(s) the Dear Healthcare Professional letter mailing(s) were sent and number of healthcare professionals that were sent this letterNumber of mailings returned
- Sources of the recipient lists
- Healthcare Professional (HCP) Training (each reporting period and cumulatively):Number of HCPs who have prescribed GATTEX
- Number of HCPs who completed the Post-Training Knowledge Assessment Questions
- Number of HCPs who have completed training and prescribed GATTEX
- Demographics of HCPs (by specialty type) that completed the Post-Training Knowledge Assessment Questions, to the extent possible
- Number and percent of HCP who completed the Post-Training Knowledge Assessment Questions by method of completion (on-line, fax/mail)
- Number of HCPs who completed each knowledge assessment question correctly and the number of HCPs who did not complete each post-training knowledge assessment question correctly
- Number of HCPs who were retrained
- Number of HCPs identified through specialty pharmacy dispensing data to have dispensed a patient prescription who did not complete the Post-Training Knowledge Assessment Questions (during the reporting period and cumulatively)
L36: - Number of HCPs who did not complete the Post-Training Knowledge Assessment Questions who were contacted by Shire-NPS Pharmaceuticals, Inc., and then who completed the Post-Training Knowledge Assessment Questions
- KAB surveys (to be conducted every two years beginning in 2018):
KAB surveys of prescribers', patients', and caregivers' understanding of the potential risks associated with use of GATTEX for SBS and their understanding of the recommended monitoring during treatment with GATTEX.
- Narrative summary of adverse events of interest including the risks of acceleration of neoplastic growth and enhancement of colon polyp growth, gastrointestinal obstruction, and biliary and pancreatic disorders, reporting from spontaneous sources, published literature, regulatory agencies, clinical studies and trials (clinical serious adverse events/SAEs), and solicited sources for entry into the Shire-NPS Pharmaceuticals, Inc. drug safety database.
- Utilization (each reporting period and cumulatively):A report of the number of GATTEX prescriptions
- A report of the number of patients who were dispensed GATTEX
- The requirements for assessments of an approved REMS under section 505-1(g)(3) include, with respect to each goal included in the strategy, an assessment of the extent to which the approved strategy, including each element of the strategy, is meeting the goal or whether one or more such goals or such elements should be modified.
