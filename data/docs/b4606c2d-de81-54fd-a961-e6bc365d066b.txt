L17: ## DATING PERIOD
L18: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Orladeyo (berotralstat) Capsules shall be 30 months from the date of manufacture when stored at controlled room temperature.
L19: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L20: Your application for Orladeyo was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because this drug is not the first in its class and the application did not raise significant safety or efficacy issues that were unexpected for a drug of this class or for this indication.
L21: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L22: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L23: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
