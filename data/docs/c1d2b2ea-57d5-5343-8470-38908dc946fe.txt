L13: Information on submitting SPL files using eList may be found in the guidance for industry titled
L14: “SPL Standard for Content of Labeling Technical Qs and As” at http://www.fda.gov/downloads/Drugs/GuidanceComplianceRegulatoryInformation/Guidances/UCM072392.pdf.

L19: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT
L20: We have received your submission dated August 22, 2018, reporting on and containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the May 10, 2006, approval letter:
L21: 1544-2 To conduct a study to determine whether varenicline, as part of an overall smoking cessation program, is effective in achieving and maintaining smoking cessation in tobacco-addicted adolescents, ages 12 through 16, inclusive, to determine a safe and effective dose, and to document the ability of treating physicians to select appropriate patients. You will need to develop a means for determining reliable criteria for appropriate patient selection of tobacco-addicted teens so that teenage smokers who are not addicted will not be recruited, and so that labeling can convey these criteria to physicians who may wish to use the drug in adolescents.
L22: We have reviewed your submissions and conclude that the above requirement has been fulfilled.
L23: This completes all of your postmarketing requirements and postmarketing commitments acknowledged in our May 10, 2006, approval letter.
