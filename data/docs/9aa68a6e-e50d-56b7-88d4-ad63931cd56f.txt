L23: ## POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)
L24: Section 505(o)(3) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L25: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a signal of serious risk of increased exposure to Ofev (nintedanib) in patients with hepatic impairment. Since Ofev (nintedanib) is eliminated primarily by hepatic metabolism, patients with hepatic impairment may require dose adjustment to minimize the risk of serious adverse events due to increased drug exposure.
L26: Furthermore, the new pharmacovigilance system that FDA is required to establish under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L27: Finally, we have determined that only a clinical trial (rather than a nonclinical or observational study) will be sufficient to assess a signal of serious risk of increased exposure to Ofev (nintedanib) in patients with hepatic impairment.
L28: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following:
L29: 2782-1 Conduct an open-label trial to evaluate the pharmacokinetics, safety and tolerability of nintedanib in patients with hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Classification A and B) compared to healthy subjects
L30: The timetable you submitted on August 26, 2014, states that you will conduct this trial according to the following schedule:
L31: Trial Completion: March 2015
Final Report Submission: September 2015
L32: Submit the protocol to your IND 074683, with a cross-reference letter to this NDA. Submit the final report to your NDA. Prominently identify the submission with the following wording in bold capital letters at the top of the first page of the submission, as appropriate: “**Required Postmarketing Protocol Under 505(o), “Required Postmarketing Final Report Under 505(o), “Required Postmarketing Correspondence Under 505(o).”**
L33: Section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) of the FDCA requires you to report periodically on the status of any study or clinical trial required under this section. This section also requires you to periodically report to FDA on the status of any study or clinical trial otherwise undertaken to investigate a safety issue. Section 506B of the FDCA, as well as 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii) requires you to report annually on the status of any postmarketing commitments or required studies or clinical trials.
L34: FDA will consider the submission of your annual report under section 506B and 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii) to satisfy the periodic reporting requirement under section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) provided that you include the elements listed in 505(o) and 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii). We remind you that to comply with 505(o), your annual report must also include a report on the status of any study or clinical trial otherwise undertaken to investigate a
L35: safety issue. Failure to submit an annual report for studies or clinical trials required under 505(o) on the date required will be considered a violation of FDCA section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) and could result in enforcement action.
