L15: ## **DATING PERIOD**
L16: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Klisyri (tirbanibulin) ointment, 1% shall be 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored at at room temperatures, 20°C – 25°C (68°F – 77°F).
L17: ## **ADVISORY COMMITTEE**
L18: Your application for Klisyri was not referred to an FDA advisory committee as there were no complex or substantive review issues identified during the application review.
L19: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study(ies) requirement for this application because actinic keratosis is an adult-related condition that rarely or never occurs in pediatrics, and studies in the pediatric population would be impossible or highly impractical to conduct.
