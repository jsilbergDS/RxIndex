L5: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for changes to the INDICATIONS AND USAGE section of the prescribing information by removing the limitations of use and adding a new indication, specifically, use for selection of patients who are indicated for amyloid beta-directed therapy as described in the prescribing information of the therapeutic products. L6: We also reference the "Changes Being Effected" sNDA-034, dated and received April 18, 2025, which provides for a new phone number for customers to report suspected adverse reactions to Neuraceq in the US Prescribing Information.

L15: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. The proposed efficacy supplement is for Alzheimer's disease, which is included in the list of adult-related conditions that rarely or never occur in the pediatric population, for which studies would be impossible or highly impractical.
