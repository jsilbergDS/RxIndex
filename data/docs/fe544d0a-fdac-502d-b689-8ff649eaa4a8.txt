L17: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to &lt;2 years for this indication. This is because it is not clear that pediatric patients with CKD at risk of progression can be easily identified in this age group because of ongoing kidney maturation and the increase in GFR that occurs after birth until approximately 2 years of age.
L20: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for this application for pediatric patients 2 to 17 years of age because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
L21: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the FDCA is a required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA. This required study is listed below.
L22: 4508-1 Conduct pharmacokinetic, efficacy, and safety assessments in patients 2 to 17 years of age with chronic kidney disease (CKD)
