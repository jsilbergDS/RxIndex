L14: This new drug application provides for the use of Januvia (sitagliptin phosphate) Tablets as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus as monotherapy and in combination with metformin or a PPAR\gamma agonist (e.g., thiazolidinediones) when diet and exercise plus the single agent do not provide adequate glycemic control.
L15: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the agreed-upon labeling text.
