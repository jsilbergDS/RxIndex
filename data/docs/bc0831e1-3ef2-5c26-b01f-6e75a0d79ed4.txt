L19: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to 9 years (inclusive) for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because there are too few children in this age range with type 2 diabetes mellitus to study.
L22: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for ages 10 to 17 years (inclusive) for dulaglutide .75 mg and 1.5 mg.
L23: We remind you that there is a PREA postmarketing requirement listed in the September 3, 2020, S-036 approval letter that is still open.
