L16: BEST PHARMACEUTICALS FOR CHILDREN ACT
L17: Section 505A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 355c), also known as the Best Pharmaceuticals for Children Act (BPCA), allows the FDA to request pediatric studies for new drugs before approval.
L18: These supplemental applications provide for pediatric labeling pursuant to BPCA. This product was studied for use in pediatric patients ages 5 to 17 years for the treatment of irritability associated with autism spectrum disorder.
