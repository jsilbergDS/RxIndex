L7: These Prior Approval sBLAs provide for expansion of the indication to include the treatment of generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) in adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor antibody (AChR-Ab) seronegative. Therefore, the indication has been revised to remove the prior qualifier of adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody positive to expand the indication to the treatment of generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) in adult patients.
