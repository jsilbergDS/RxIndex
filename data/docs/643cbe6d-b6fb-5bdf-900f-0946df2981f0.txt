L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received June 14, 2024, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Vitrakvi (larotrectinib), capsules.
L5: This Prior Approval sNDA provides an update to the Vitrakvi (larotrectinib) Prescribing Information (PI) and Patient Information with efficacy and safety results from three clinical studies: Study 20288, Study 20289 (NAVIGATE) and Study 20290 (SCOUT), supporting the conversion from accelerated approval to regular approval for the treatment of adult and pediatric patients with solid tumors that:
L6: - have a neurotrophic receptor tyrosine kinase (NTRK) gene fusion without a known acquired resistance mutation,
- are metastatic or where surgical resection is likely to result in severe morbidity, and
- have no satisfactory alternative treatments or that have progressed following treatment.

L22: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT
L23: We have received your submission dated June 14, 2024, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the November 26, 2018, approval letter.
L24: 3540-1 Submit the final report, including datasets, from ongoing and proposed trials conducted to verify and describe the clinical benefit of larotrectinib, through more precise estimation of the overall response rate and mature response duration per independent review assessment, in adult and pediatric patients with solid tumors with a neurotrophic receptor tyrosine kinase (NTRK) gene fusion and without a known acquired resistance mutation; are metastatic or where surgical resection is likely to result in severe morbidity; and have no satisfactory alternative treatment or that have progressed following treatment. A sufficient number of patients will be evaluated to characterize response and durability of response for each of the following tumor types: colorectal cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, central nervous system tumors, and melanoma. A minimum of 40 patients with cancers other than colorectal cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, central nervous system tumors, melanoma, soft tissue sarcoma, thyroid cancer, infantile fibrosarcoma, and salivary cancers (e.g., breast cancer, gastrointestinal stromal tumors, cholangiocarcinoma, biliary tract cancers) will also be studied. Overall response rate and duration of response will be assessed by independent central review and all responding patients will be followed for at least 12 months from the onset of response.
L25: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above requirement was fulfilled.
