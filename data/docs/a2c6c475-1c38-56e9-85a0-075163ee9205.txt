L17: BEST PHARMACEUTICALS FOR CHILDREN ACT
L18: Section 505A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 355c), also known as the Best Pharmaceuticals for Children Act (BPCA), allows the FDA to request pediatric studies for new drugs before approval.
L19: This supplemental application provides for pediatric labeling pursuant to BPCA. This supplemental application is approving use in pediatric patients ages 10 years and older who weigh 25 kilograms or more, for the treatment of relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis. This approval is in response to a Written Request.
L20: REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L21: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L22: Because none of these criteria apply to your supplement application, you are exempt from this requirement.
L23: FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT
L24: We have received your submissions dated November 10, 2025, and November 11, 2025, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the September 15, 2023, postapproval postmarketing requirement letter for BLA 761053.
L25: 3194-14 Conduct a two-part study of ocrelizumab in pediatric patients with relapsing multiple sclerosis (RMS) at least 10 years and less than 17 years of age. Part A is an open-label study of the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics (PK), and pharmacodynamics (PD) of ocrelizumab in pediatric patients weighing 40 kg or less. The objective of Part A is to determine a dose of ocrelizumab that will result in PK and PD effects that are comparable to those of a 600 mg dose (300 mg given twice 14 days
L26: apart) in adult patients with RMS. Safety assessments will continue for at least 2 years after the last dose of ocrelizumab. Part B is a randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of ocrelizumab compared to an appropriate comparator.
L27: We have reviewed your submissions and conclude that the above requirement has been fulfilled.
L28: We remind you that there are postmarketing requirements listed in the March 28, 2017, approval letter that are still open.
