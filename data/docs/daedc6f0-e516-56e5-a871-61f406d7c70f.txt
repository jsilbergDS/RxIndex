L7: ## LICENSING
L8: We have approved your BLA for Nexviazyme (avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt) effective this date. You are hereby authorized to introduce or deliver for introduction into interstate commerce, Nexviazyme under your existing Department of Health and Human Services U.S. License No. 1596. Nexviazyme is indicated for the treatment of patients 1 year of age and older with late-onset Pompe disease (lysosomal acid alpha-glucosidase [GAA] deficiency).
L9: ## MANUFACTURING LOCATIONS
L10: Under this license, you are approved to manufacture avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt drug substance at Genzyme Flanders bvba in Geel, Belgium. The final formulated drug product will be manufactured, filled, labeled, and packaged at Genzyme Ireland Limited, Waterford, Ireland; secondary packaged and labeled at Genzyme Corporation, Northborough, MA. You may label your product with the proprietary name, Nexviazyme, and market it in 100mg/vial powder for injection.
L11: ## DATING PERIOD
L12: The dating period for Nexviazyme (avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt) shall be 48 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 5\pm 3 °C. The date of manufacture shall be defined as the date of final sterile filtration of the formulated drug product. The dating period for your drug substance shall be (b)(4) months from the date of manufacture when stored at (b)(4) °C.

L30: ## POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)
L31: Section 505(o)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L32: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to identify unexpected serious risks of pregnancy and maternal complications, adverse effects on the developing fetus and neonate, and adverse effects on the infant in females of child-bearing potential who are prescribed Nexviazyme (avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt) during pregnancy and/or lactation.
L33: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks.
L34: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following study:
L35: 4026-1 Conduct a worldwide, descriptive safety study that collects data in women and their offspring who are exposed to Nexviazyme (avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt) during pregnancy and/or lactation to assess risk of pregnancy and maternal complications, adverse effects on the developing fetus and neonate, and adverse effects on the infant. Outcomes of exposed infants,
L36: including growth and development, will be assessed through at least the first year of life. The study will collect information for 10 years.
