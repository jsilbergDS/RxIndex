L9: Please refer to your April 17, 2024, supplemental biologics license application (sBLA), submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Vectibix (panitumumab) injection.
L10: We acknowledge receipt of your major amendment dated September 16, 2024, which extended the goal date by three months.
L11: This Prior Approval supplemental biologics license application adds a new indication of Vectibix, in combination with sotorasib, for the treatment of adult patients with KRAS G12C-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), as determined by an FDA approved test, who have received prior treatment with fluoropyrimidine-, oxaliplatin-, and irinotecan-based chemotherapy. It also adds the following statement to the Limitations of Use: "Vectibix is not indicated for the treatment of patients with RAS-mutant mCRC unless used in combination with sotorasib in KRAS G12C-mutated mCRC."
L12: In addition, this supplement provides for updates to the U.S. Prescribing Information (USPI) in Section 2, Dosage and Administration, Section 5, Warnings and Precautions, and Section 16, How Supplied/Storage and Handling.

L22: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L23: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L24: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable because CRC is an adult-related condition that rarely occurs in the pediatric population.
