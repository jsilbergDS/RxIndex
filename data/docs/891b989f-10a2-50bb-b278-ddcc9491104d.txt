L17: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for less than 12 years of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because inadequately controlled CRSwNP is rare in this age group. In general, nasal polyps in this age group occur secondary to other disease states, such as cystic fibrosis, primary ciliary dyskinesia, or immunodeficiency.
