L5: This Prior Approval sNDA provides for updates to the Prescribing Information based on the final results of Clinical Trial C2302 entitled, "A Phase 3, multi-center, randomized, double-blind, 48 week study with an initial 12 week placebo-controlled period to evaluate the safety and efficacy of osilodrostat in patients with Cushing's disease", as well as safety updates on febrile neutropenia. In addition, the Patient Package Insert was revised to improve safe use of the product.

L14: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L15: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L16: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
