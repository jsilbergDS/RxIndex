L17: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We reference the waiver, previously granted on August 3, 2017 for the pediatric study requirement for ages birth to less than 3 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable.
L20: This product is appropriately labeled for use in pediatric patients 12 to less than 18 years of age for this indication. Therefore, no additional studies are needed in this pediatric age group.

L24: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the FDCA is a required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the FDCA. This required study is listed below.
L25: 3719-1 Conduct a study to evaluate the pharmacokinetics, safety and treatment response (using sustained virologic response) of glecaprevir and pibrentasvir in pediatric subjects 3 through less than 12 years of age with chronic hepatitis C virus infection.
L26: Final Protocol Submission: January 2017 (submitted)
L27: Study Completion: July 31, 2022
L28: Final Report Submission: January 31, 2023
