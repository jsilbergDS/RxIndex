L7: - autologous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT) and brentuximab vedotin, or

L14: ## ACCELERATED APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
L15: Products approved under the accelerated approval regulations, 21 CFR 601.41, require further adequate and well-controlled studies/clinical trials to verify and describe clinical benefit. You are required to conduct such studies/clinical trials with due diligence. If postmarketing studies/clinical trials fail to verify clinical benefit or are not conducted with due diligence, we may, following a hearing in accordance with 21 CFR 601.43(b), withdraw this approval. We remind you of the following postmarketing requirement (PMR) listed in the May 17, 2016, approval letter:
L16: 3089-1 Conduct a randomized phase 3 clinical trial in classical Hodgkin lymphoma that verifies and isolates the clinical benefit of nivolumab for patients with classical Hodgkin lymphoma. The primary endpoint would be progression-free survival as determined by an independent review committee. Overall survival would be a key secondary endpoint.
L17: Trial Completion: 06/2021
Primary Progression-Free Survival Analysis: 09/2024
Final Report Submission: 12/2026
L18: Fulfillment of PMR 3089-1 may also fulfill your requirement under this supplement.
L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: Because this biologic product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
