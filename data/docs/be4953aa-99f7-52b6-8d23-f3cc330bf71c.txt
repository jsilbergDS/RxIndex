L10: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for an expansion of the indication of Trikafta (ELX/TEZ/IVA) tablets and granules for the treatment of cystic fibrosis (CF) in adult and pediatric patients aged 2 years and older who have a clinical diagnosis of CF and who have at least one variant in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene that is either responsive based on clinical and/or in vitro data or results in production of CFTR protein. This indication expansion is based on 265 ultra-rare CFTR variants included in Section 12 of the Prescribing Information.
