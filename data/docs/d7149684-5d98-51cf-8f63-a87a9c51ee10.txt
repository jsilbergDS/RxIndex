Both panels share the same axes: Time (minutes) on the X-axis (0 to 65) and Cumulative Amount Dissolved (% of label amount) on the Y-axis (50 to 110). The data points are obscured by redaction blocks labeled (b) (4).
L767: Figure 39-3: Dissolution profile of 80 mg tablets. The graph shows Dissolution (%) on the Y-axis (0 to 100) versus Time (minutes) on the X-axis (0 to 60). The dissolution curve rises sharply and reaches 100% dissolution within 5 minutes, indicating rapid dissolution.
