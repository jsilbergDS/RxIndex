L8: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received July 29, 2021, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Mekinist (trametinib) tablet.
L9: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application revises the CLINICAL STUDIES, BRAFT V600E Mutation-Positive Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (14.3) subsection with additional follow-up for duration of response (DOR) in patients who received dabrafenib as a single agent or trametinib in combination with dabrafenib and were considered responders in Study BRF113928 at the time of the initial approval. Additionally, the overall response rate (ORR) results and categorizations of best overall response were updated for patients who were previously treated and received trametinib in combination with dabrafenib. Similarly, the categorizations of best overall response were updated for patients who were treatment-naive prior to receiving trametinib in combination with dabrafenib.
