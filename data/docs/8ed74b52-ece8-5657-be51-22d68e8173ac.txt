L34: ## DATING PERIOD
L35: Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Gemtesa (vibegron) tablets shall be 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 20°C to 25°C.
L36: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L37: Your application for Gemtesa was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because outside expertise was not necessary; there were no controversial issues that would benefit from advisory committee discussion.
L38: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L39: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L40: We are waiving this requirement for ages 0 to less than 3 years of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable.
L41: We are deferring submission of your pediatric trial in patients 3 to less than 17 years of age because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric trial has not yet been completed.
L42: Your deferred pediatric trial required under section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act is a required postmarketing trial. The status of this postmarketing trial must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. This required trial is listed below.
L43: #3991-1 A sequential cohort, vebegron dose-modification trial to determine the multiple-dose pharmacokinetics, safety and efficacy of vebegron in the treatment of pediatric patients with neurogenic detrusor overactivity aged 3 to less than 17 years.
L44: Final Protocol Submission: June 2021
L45: Study Initiation: April 2022
L46: Study Completion: September 2027
L47: Final Report Submission: March 2028
