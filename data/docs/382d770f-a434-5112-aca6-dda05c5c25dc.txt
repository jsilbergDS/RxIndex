L5: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated and received November 7, 2024, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for ZEGFROVY (sunvozertinib) tablets.
L6: This NDA provides for the use of ZEGFROVY (sunvozertinib) tablet for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) exon 20 insertion mutations, as detected by an FDA-approved test, whose disease has progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy.

L40: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L41: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L42: We are waiving the pediatric study(ies) requirement for this application because it would be highly impracticable or infeasible to conduct studies in the pediatric population in which this drug may exert meaningful activity due to the rarity of EGFR sensitizing mutations or uncommon mutations, T790M mutation, Exon20ins mutations, and HER2 mutations in pediatric patients.

L54: A drug with a name recognized in the official United States Pharmacopeia or official National Formulary (USP-NF) generally must comply with the compendial standards for strength, quality, and purity, unless the difference in strength, quality, or purity is plainly stated on its label (see FD&amp;C Act § 501(b), 21 USC 351(b)). To help ensure that a drug continues to comply with compendial standards, application holders may work directly with USP-NF to revise official USP monographs.
