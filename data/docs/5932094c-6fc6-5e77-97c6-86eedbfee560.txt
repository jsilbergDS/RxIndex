L4: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received August 03, 2022, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Lampit (nifurtimox) tablets, 30 mg and 120 mg.
L5: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application proposes conversion of Lampit from accelerated approval to full approval for use in pediatric patients (birth to less than 18 years of age and weighing at least 2.5 kg) for the treatment of Chagas disease (American Trypanosomiasis), caused by *Trypanosoma cruzi*. Specifically, the following sections of the prescribing information have been revised: **HIGHLIGHTS OF PRESCRIBING INFORMATION, INDICATIONS AND USAGE (1), USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS (8) and CLINICAL STUDIES (14), and a REFERENCES (15)** section has been added. Additionally, minor revisions to the **PATIENT INFORMATION** have been included.
