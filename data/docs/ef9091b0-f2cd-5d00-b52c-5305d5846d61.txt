L25: We have received your submission dated December 5, 2025, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the October 25, 2022, approval letter for BLA 761291.
L26: 4334-1 Conduct a randomized clinical trial in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. The trial should enroll sufficient numbers of racial and ethnic minority patients and older patients (ages 65-74 and 75 and above) to enable an evaluation of teclistamab in a study population that better reflects the U.S. population of patients with multiple myeloma. Patients should be randomized to receive a teclistamab-based regimen compared to standard therapy for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. The primary endpoint should be progression-free survival and secondary endpoints should include overall survival, overall response rate, and duration of response.
L27: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above requirement was fulfilled.

L29: RISK EVALUATION AND MITIGATION STRATEGY (REMS) REQUIREMENTS
L30: The REMS for Tecvayli (teclistamab-cqyv) was originally approved on October 25, 2022, and the combined Tecvayli and Talvey REMS was approved on August 9, 2023. The most recent REMS modification was approved on October 2, 2025. The REMS consists of a communication plan, elements to assure safe use, an implementation system, and a timetable for submission of assessments of the REMS.
L31: Your proposed modifications to the REMS consist of removal of the communication plan as an element of the REMS and updates to the REMS materials to reflect the revisions to the indication statement and the hospitalization recommendation during step-up dosing in the Tecvayli (teclistamab-cqyv) U.S. Prescribing Information.
L32: Communication Plan: We have determined that the communication plan is no longer necessary as an element of the REMS to ensure the benefits of Tecvayli (teclistamab-cqyv) and Talvey (talquetamab-tgvs) outweigh their risks because the communication plan has been completed. Additionally, the Agency's completed review of the 2-year REMS assessment report found that the REMS was meeting its goals and that you disseminated the REMS communication materials to the target audience as required. No further assessments are necessary to assess the current communication plan.
