L22: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L23: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L24: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
L25: ## FULFILLMENT OF REQUIRED POSTMARKETING STUDY COMMITMENT
L26: We have received your submission dated August 2, 2017, containing the final report for the following “required postmarketing study commitment” listed in our May 10, 2006, NDA Approval Letter;
L27: 1544-3 To conduct a prospective epidemiologic cohort study in pregnant women who are smokers and who are exposed to varenicline at the time of conception or any time
L28: during pregnancy. This information will be used to assess the potential risk to the fetus and/or live born infant.
L29: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above required postmarketing study commitment has been fulfilled.
L30: ## **FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT**
L31: We have received your submission dated August 2, 2017, containing the final report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in our September 22, 2011, New Postmarketing Requirement letter.
L32: 1544-5 Conduct an extension of the randomized, double-blind, active- and placebo controlled trial to compare the risk of clinically significant neuropsychiatric events in individuals using Chantix (varenicline), bupropion, nicotine replacement therapy, or placebo, as aids to smoking cessation. The non-treatment, 28-week extension trial will be aimed at collecting and comparing data on cardiovascular safety for all participants, allowing for a total of 52 weeks of cardiovascular safety data collection.
L33: We have reviewed your submission and conclude that the above requirement has been fulfilled.
L34: We remind you that there is a required postmarketing study commitment listed in the May 10, 2006, letter, that is still open.
