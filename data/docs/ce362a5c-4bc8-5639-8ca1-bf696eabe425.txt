L5: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated May 24, 2023, received May 24, 2023, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Brilinta (ticagrelor) tablets.
L6: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for revisions to labeling to provide guidance to prescribers regarding discontinuation of concomitant aspirin in patients with coronary artery disease who have undergone percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI). Throughout the prescribing information, language advising not to exceed 100 mg of maintenance dose of aspirin has been revised because the recommended dose of concomitant aspirin is clearly stated for each indication.

L15: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
