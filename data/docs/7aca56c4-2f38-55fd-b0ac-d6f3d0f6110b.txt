L9: This NDA provides for the use of Filspari (sparsentan) tablets to reduce proteinuria in adults with primary immunoglobulin A nephropathy (IgAN) at risk of rapid disease progression, generally a urine protein to creatinine ratio (UPCR) \ge 1.5 g/g.

L68: In accordance with section 505-1 of FDCA, we have determined that a REMS is necessary for Filspari (sparsentan) to ensure the benefits of the drug outweigh the risks of embryo-fetal toxicity and hepatotoxicity.
