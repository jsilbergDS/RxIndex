L13: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L14: Your application for Seysara (sarecycline hydrochloride) tablets, 60 mg, 100 mg and 150 mg was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because the application did not raise significant public health questions on the role of the drug in the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of a disease.
L15: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages 0 to 9 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because prepubertal acne at the age of younger than 9 years is not common.
L18: This product is appropriately labeled for use in ages 9 to 17 years for this indication. Therefore, no additional studies are needed in this pediatric group.
L19: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for ages 9 to 17 years for this application.
