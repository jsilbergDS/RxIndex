L6: This new drug application provides for the use of SPRYCEL™ (dasatinib) Tablets for the treatment of adults with chronic myeloid leukemia with resistance or intolerance to prior therapy including imatinib.
L7: We completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved under the provisions of accelerated approval regulations (21 CFR 314.510), effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed labeling text and patient labeling. Marketing of this drug product and related activities must adhere to the substance and procedures of the referenced accelerated approval regulations.
