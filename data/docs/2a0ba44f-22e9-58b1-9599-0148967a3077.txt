L6: ## LICENSING
L7: We are issuing Department of Health and Human Services U.S. License No. 2237 to Hugel, Inc., Chuncheon, Gangwon, Korea under the provisions of section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act controlling the manufacture and sale of biological products. The license authorizes you to introduce or deliver for introduction into interstate commerce, those products for which your company has demonstrated compliance with establishment and product standards.
L8: Under this license, you are authorized to manufacture the product Letybo (letibotulinumtoxinA-wlbg) for injection, for intramuscular use indicated for the temporary improvement in the appearance of moderate to severe glabellar lines associated with corrugator and/or procerus muscle activity in adult patients.
L9: ## MANUFACTURING LOCATIONS
L10: Under this license, you are approved to manufacture letibotulinumtoxinA-wlbg at your facility in Chuncheon-si, Gangwon-do, Republic of Korea. You may label your product with the proprietary name, Letybo, and market it in 50 Units/vial or 100 Units/vial for injection.
L11: ## **DATING PERIOD**
L12: The dating period for Letybo shall be 36 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 2- 8 °C. The date of manufacture shall be defined as the date of final sterile filtration of the formulated drug product. The dating period for your drug substance shall be (b) (4) months from the date of manufacture when stored at (b) (4) °C.
L13: ## **FDA LOT RELEASE**
L14: You are not currently required to submit samples of future lots of Letybo to the Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) for release by the Director, CDER, under 21 CFR 610.2. We will continue to monitor compliance with 21 CFR 610.1, requiring completion of tests for conformity with standards applicable to each product prior to release of each lot.
L15: Any changes in the manufacturing, testing, packaging, or labeling of Letybo, or in the manufacturing facilities, will require the submission of information to your BLA for our review and written approval, consistent with 21 CFR 601.12.

L28: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L29: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L30: We are waiving the pediatric study(ies) requirement for this application because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable in patients less than age 18 years of age.
