L12: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L13: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application since studies are impossible or highly impracticable because Diabetic Macular Edema rarely occurs in the pediatric population.
