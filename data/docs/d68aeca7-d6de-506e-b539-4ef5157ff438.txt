L19: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study(ies) requirement for ages 0 to less than 4 weeks of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because with improvement in perinatal transmission prevention strategies there are insufficient numbers of neonatal subjects to be enrolled. Further, even when neonates are identified for enrollment, by the time enrollment is accomplished, dosing is initiated, and drug concentrations have reached steady state, the subjects are likely to be older than 4 weeks of age.
L22: This product is appropriately labeled for use in treatment-naïve and treatment-experienced, INSTI-naïve patients weighing at least 30 kg for this indication. Therefore, no additional studies are needed in this pediatric group.
L23: We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for treatment-naïve and treatment-experienced, INSTI-naïve patients weighing 14 kg to less than 30 kg with this application.
