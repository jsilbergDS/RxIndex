L4: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated September 29, 2019, received, September 30, 2019, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Tavuid (Flortaucipir F18) Injection.
L5: This new drug application provides for the use of Tavuid (Flortaucipir F18) Injection as a radioactive diagnostic agent indicated for positron emission tomography (PET) imaging of the brain to estimate the density and distribution of aggregated tau neurofibrillary tangles (NFTs) in adult patients with cognitive impairment who are being evaluated for Alzheimer's disease (AD).
L6: ## APPROVAL &amp; LABELING
L7: We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.
