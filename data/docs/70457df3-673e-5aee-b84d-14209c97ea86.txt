L18: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS FOR sBLA 125360/086
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.

L22: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT FOR sBLA 125360/086
L23: We have reviewed your submission dated June 16, 2020, reporting on the following postmarketing requirement listed in the July 3, 2018, approval letter for sBLA 125360/073.
L24: 3443-1 A double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-control study in patients ages 6 to 17 years, and an open-label, single-group, non-controlled study in patients ages 2 to 5 years conducted under PREA to evaluate the efficacy and safety of Xeomin for treatment of chronic troublesome sialorrhea in the pediatric population. This will be followed by an open-label extension period to investigate the safety of repeat dosing of Xeomin for the treatment of pediatric patients (2-17 years) with chronic troublesome sialorrhea.
