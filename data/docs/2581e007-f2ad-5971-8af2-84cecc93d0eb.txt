L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for children weighing less than 40 kg because the product does not represent a meaningful therapeutic benefit over existing therapies for pediatric patients in this age group **and** is not likely to be used in a substantial number of pediatric patients in this age group.
L22: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages less than 18 years and weighing at least 40 kg for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
L23: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA is a required postmarketing study. The status of this postmarketing study must be reported annually according to 21 CFR 314.81 and section 505B(a)(4)(C) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act/FDCA. This required study is listed below.
