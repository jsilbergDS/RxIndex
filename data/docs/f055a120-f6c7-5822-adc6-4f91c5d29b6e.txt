L16: ## FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT
L17: Your submissions of December 7, 2015, contain the final reports for the following postmarketing requirements listed in the October 29, 2010, approval letter.
L18: **1692-002:** Perform a randomized comparison of Teflaro (ceftaroline fosamil) and comparator in pediatric subjects with CABP utilizing an enrichment strategy for enrollment of patients with methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus* (MRSA). Pediatric patients under 17 years of age with CABP must be enrolled, with a minimum of 150 patients receiving Teflaro (ceftaroline fosamil).
L19: **1692-003:** Perform a randomized comparison of Teflaro (ceftaroline fosamil) and comparator in pediatric subjects with ABSSSI including patients with infection suspected or demonstrated to be caused by MRSA. Pediatric patients under 17 years of age with ABSSSI must be enrolled, with a minimum of 150 patients receiving Teflaro (ceftaroline fosamil).
L20: We have reviewed your submissions and conclude that the above requirements are fulfilled.
