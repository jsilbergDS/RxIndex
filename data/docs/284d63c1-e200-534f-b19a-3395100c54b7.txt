L8: - information on the use of Viekira Pak without ribavirin in GT1b-infected subjects with compensated cirrhosis;
- the deletion of the statement that Viekira Pak has not been studied in patients on dialysis and addition of the following underlined text, "No dose adjustment of VIEKIRA PAK™ is required in patients with mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment, including those on dialysis" in subsection 8.7; and
- updates for subsections 8.1-8.3 to satisfy the Pregnancy and Labeling Rule Requirement.

L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: We reference your waiver previously granted on December 19, 2014 for VIEKIRA PAK for ages birth to less than 3 years of age because the product does not represent a meaningful therapeutic benefit over existing therapies for pediatric patients in this age group and is not likely to be used in a substantial number of pediatric patients in this group.
L22: We reference your deferred pediatric studies previously granted on December 19, 2014 for VIEKIRA PAK for ages 3 to less than 18 years.
