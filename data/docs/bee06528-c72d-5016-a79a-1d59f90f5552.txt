L5: Please refer to your Supplemental Biologics License Applications (sBLAs), dated and received June 28 and 29, 2018, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Praluent (alirocumab) injection, 75 mg and 150 mg.
L6: These Prior Approval supplemental biologics applications provide for the following:
L7: - S-019 provides for a new indication to reduce the risk of myocardial infarction, stroke and unstable angina requiring hospitalization in adults with established cardiovascular disease. These changes are based on the results of the Praluent cardiovascular outcomes trial, EFC11570, titled "*A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group Study to Evaluate the Effect of Alirocumab (SAR236553/REGN727) on the Occurrence of Cardiovascular Events in Patients Who Have Recently Experienced an Acute Coronary Syndrome*", (ODYSSEY Outcomes).
- S-020 provides for broadening the lipid-lowering indication based on previously submitted clinical studies and on the results of the ODYSSEY Outcomes trial.
L8: These supplements provide for changes to the Prescribing Information and Patient Package Insert.

L17: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for Supplement-019 because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This was determined because cardiovascular disease requires years to develop and is rare in pediatric patients.
L20: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for Supplement-020 because none of these criteria apply to this supplemental application and you are exempt from this requirement.
