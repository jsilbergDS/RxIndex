L48: ## RISK EVALUATION AND MITIGATION STRATEGY REQUIREMENTS
L49: Section 505-1 of the Food Drug Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require the submission of a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy (REMS), if FDA determines that such a strategy is necessary to ensure that the benefits of the drug outweigh the risks.
L50: In accordance with section 505-1 of FDCA, we have determined that a REMS is necessary for ULTOMIRISTM (ravulizumab-cwvz) to ensure the benefits of the drug outweigh the risk of meningococcal infection.
L51: Your proposed REMS must also include the following:
L52: **Elements to assure safe use:** Pursuant to 505-1(f)(1), we have also determined that ULTOMIRIS™ (ravulizumab-cwvz) can be approved only if elements necessary to assure safe use are required as part of the REMS to mitigate the risk of meningococcal infection. Your REMS includes the following elements to mitigate this risk:
L53: - Healthcare providers have particular experience or training, or are specially certified
