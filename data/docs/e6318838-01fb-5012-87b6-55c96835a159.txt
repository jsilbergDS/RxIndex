L20: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L21: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.

L24: We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for ages birth to less than 1 year of age because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable.
L25: We are deferring submission of your pediatric study for ages 1 to less than 18 years for this application because this product is ready for approval for use in adults and the pediatric study has not been completed.
L26: Your deferred pediatric study required by section 505B(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) are required postmarketing study.
