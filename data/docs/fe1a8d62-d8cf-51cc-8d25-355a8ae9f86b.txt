L7: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for expansion of the indication for the use of tezacaftor/ivacaftor 50 mg/75 mg once daily followed by ivacaftor 75 mg 12 hours later for patients &lt; 30 kg, and tezacaftor/ivacaftor 100 mg/150 mg once daily followed by ivacaftor 150 mg 12 hours later for patients ≥ 30 kg, for the treatment of cystic fibrosis in patients aged 6 to less than 12 years old who are homozygous for the *F508del* mutation or who have at least one mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (*CFTR*) gene that is responsive to tezacaftor/ivacaftor based on *in vitro* data and/or clinical evidence.

L18: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: Because none of these criteria apply to your application, you are exempt from this requirement.
