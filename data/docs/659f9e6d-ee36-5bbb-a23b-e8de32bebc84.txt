L17: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L18: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L19: We remind you of your required post-marketing studies (PMRs) as specified in the February 25, 2015, NDA approval letter. These three studies will evaluate the use of Avycaz (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children:
L20: #2862-1 Conduct a randomized multicenter, active-controlled trial to evaluate the safety and tolerability of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children from 3
L21: months to less than 18 years of age with cUTI. The dose for this study will be determined upon review of the data to be submitted by June 2015 from a single-dose, multicenter, noncomparative study assessing the pharmacokinetics of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in pediatric patients from 3 months to less than 18 years of age.
L22: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2015
L23: Study Completion: 09/2017
L24: Final Report Submission: 09/2018
L25: #2862-2 Conduct a randomized, multicenter, active-controlled trial to evaluate the safety and tolerability of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children from 3 months to less than 18 years of age with cIAI. The dose for this study will be determined upon review of the data to be submitted by June 2015 from a single-dose, multicenter, noncomparative study assessing the pharmacokinetics of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in pediatric patients from 3 months to less than 18 years of age.
L26: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2015
L27: Study Completion: 09/2017
L28: Final Report Submission: 09/2018
L29: #2862-3 Conduct a trial to evaluate the pharmacokinetics, safety and tolerability of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children from birth to less than 3 months of age with late-onset sepsis.
L30: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2018
L31: Study Completion: 12/2019
L32: Final Report Submission: 12/2020
