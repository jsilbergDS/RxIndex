L17: ## ADVISORY COMMITTEE
L18: Your application for Ekterly was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because there are no major issues regarding the efficacy or safety data submitted in this application that require external discussion at an AC meeting. The proposed product will provide an alternative treatment for acute HAE attacks, but does not represent a novel therapeutic drug class.
L19: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS
L20: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L21: Because this drug product for this indication has an orphan drug designation, you are exempt from this requirement.
L22: ## POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)
L23: Section 505(o)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L24: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to identify an unexpected serious risk of tumorigenicity to patients with HAE who take sebeltralstat long-term.
L25: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L26: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following study:
L27: 4814-1 Conduct a 2-year carcinogenicity study in rats with sebetalstat

Failure to submit an annual report for studies or clinical trials required under 505(o) on the date required will be considered a violation of FDCA section 505(o)(3)(E)(ii) and could result in enforcement action.

L45: ## **COMPENDIAL STANDARDS**
L46: A drug with a name recognized in the official United States Pharmacopeia or official National Formulary (USP-NF) generally must comply with the compendial standards for strength, quality, and purity, unless the difference in strength, quality, or purity is plainly stated on its label (see FD&amp;C Act § 501(b), 21 USC 351(b)). FDA typically cannot share application-specific information contained in submitted regulatory filings with third parties, which includes USP-NF. To help ensure that a drug continues to comply with compendial standards, application holders may work directly with USP-NF to revise official USP monographs.
