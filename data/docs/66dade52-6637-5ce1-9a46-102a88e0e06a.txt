L28: ## **POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o)**
L29: Section 505(o)(3) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute.
L30: We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a signal of serious risk of the development of resistance as a result of sequence variation in the SARS-CoV-2 replicase proteins that interact with remdesivir that could lead to potential transmission of remdesivir-resistant SARS-CoV-2 variants.
L31: Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess this serious risk.
L32: Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following study:
L33: 4443-1 Evaluate the impact of the following substitutions identified in subjects from Study GS-US-540-5912 on remdesivir susceptibility using the subgenomic replicon system: nsp12: E136K, R181C, and T801I; nsp13: V6F, T127I, E136D, Y253H, S485L, and Y541C; Combination assessments: nsp12 E136K+T801I and nsp12_E136K+nsp12_T801I+nsp13_Y253H. Assessments should include substitutions associated with resistance spanning the range of susceptibilities as references.
L34: The timetable you submitted on April 21, 2023, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L35: Study Completion: 12/2023
Final Report Submission: 01/2024
L36: FDA considers the term *final* to mean that the applicant has submitted a protocol, the FDA review team has sent comments to the applicant, and the protocol has been revised as needed to meet the goal of the study or clinical trial.3
