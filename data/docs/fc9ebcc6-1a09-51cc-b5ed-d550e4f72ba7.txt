L6: This new drug application provides for the use of VEKLURY (remdesivir) injection, and VEKLURY(remdesivir) for injection, for adults and pediatric patients (12 years of age and older and weighing at least 40 kg) for the treatment of coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) requiring hospitalization. VEKLURY should only be administered in a hospital or in a healthcare setting capable of providing acute care comparable to inpatient hospital care.

L18: ## **DATING PERIOD**
L19: Based on the data provided, the expiration dating period for VEKLURY (remdesivir) injection is 12 months when stored between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Based on the data provided, the expiration dating period for VEKLURY (remdesivir) for injection is 30 months when stored below 30°C (below 86°F).
