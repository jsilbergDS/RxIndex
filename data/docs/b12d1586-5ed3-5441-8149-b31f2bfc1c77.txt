L7: “As only limited clinical safety and efficacy data for AVYCAZ are currently available, reserve AVYCAZ for use in patients who have limited or no alternative treatment options.”

L18: ## REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENT
L19: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L20: We remind you of your previous PREA post-marketing requirements (PMRs) specified in our February 25, 2015, original NDA approval letter. We note that the following three studies will evaluate the use of Avycaz (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children:
L21: - 2862-1 Conduct a randomized multicenter, active-controlled trial to evaluate the safety and tolerability of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children from 3 months to less than 18 years of age with cUTI. The dose for this study will be determined upon review of the data to be submitted by June 2015 from a single-dose, multicenter, non-comparative study assessing the pharmacokinetics of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in pediatric patients from 3 months to less than 18 years of age.
L22: The timetable you submitted on February 11, 2015, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L23: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2015
Study Completion: 09/2017
Final Report Submission: 09/2018
L24: - 2862-2 Conduct a randomized, multicenter, active-controlled trial to evaluate the safety and tolerability of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children from 3 months to less than 18 years of age with cIAI. The dose for this study will be determined upon review of the data to be submitted by June 2015 from a single-dose, multicenter, non-comparative study assessing the pharmacokinetics of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in pediatric patients from 3 months to less than 18 years of age.
L25: The timetable you submitted on February 11, 2015, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L26: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2015
Study Completion: 09/2017
Final Report Submission: 09/2018
L27: - 2862-3 Conduct a trial to evaluate the pharmacokinetics, safety and tolerability of AVYCAZ (ceftazidime and avibactam) in children from birth to less than 3 months of age with late-onset sepsis.
L28: The timetable you submitted on February 11, 2015, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule:
L29: Final Protocol Submission: 06/2018
Study Completion: 12/2019
Final Report Submission: 12/2020
L30: Submit the protocols to your IND 101307 with a cross-reference letter to NDA 206494.
L31: Reports of these required pediatric post-marketing studies must be submitted as a new drug application (NDA) or as a supplement to your approved NDA with the proposed labeling changes you believe are warranted based on the data derived from these studies.
L32: When submitting the reports, please clearly mark your submission **"SUBMISSION OF REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS"** in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the submission.
L33: We also remind you that there are other post-marketing requirements and post-marketing commitments listed in our February 26, 2015, approval letter that are still open.
