L6: We acknowledge receipt of your amendments dated March 31, 2014, May 6, 2014, June 9, 2014, June 18, 2014, September 11, 2014, November 25, 2014 and December 9, 2014.
L7: This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application updates the Indications and Usage section of the Prescribing Information (PI) to include patients who are virologically-suppressed (HIV-1 RNA &lt;50 copies/mL) on a stable antiretroviral regimen for at least 6 months with no history of treatment failure in order to replace their current regimen. Patients should have no known substitutions associated with resistance to the individual components of STRIBILD.
