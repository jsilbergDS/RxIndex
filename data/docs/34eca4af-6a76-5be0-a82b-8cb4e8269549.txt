L15: ## **REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS**
L16: Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients, new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
L17: We are granting a partial waiver from birth through less than 6 years of age based on evidence strongly suggesting studies are impossible or highly impracticable in this pediatric subpopulation based on diagnostic criteria and assessment measures for determining efficacy for the treatment of ADHD in children less than six years not being well defined, and pharmacological treatment being uncommon.
L18: In addition, for patients 6 years to 17 years, an adequate assessment has been submitted based on the extrapolation of efficacy from adult maintenance studies and the extrapolation of long-term safety from longer-term pediatric safety studies. Therefore, no additional pediatric studies are needed at this time.
